What in the world does this mean? Laura wondered, but her attention was already captured by Professor Aurelius Morgenstern, who began to sing a peculiar tune. His voice, which had sounded so dull before, now rang out with power and clarity. His song filled the room with an enchanting melody. And even though Laura did not understand the verses, which were in a foreign language she had never heard before, emanating from them was an unusual strength. A strange calm settled over Laura, and suddenly she felt a deep, unfamiliar sense of confidence. She knew now that everything would turn out well.
