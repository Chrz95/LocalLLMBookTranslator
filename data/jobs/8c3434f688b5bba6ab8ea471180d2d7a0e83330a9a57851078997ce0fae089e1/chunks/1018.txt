How could this have been possible? How had she returned from the professor's house to her room? She had not the slightest idea. Suddenly, the events of the past night came flooding back to her, but everything that had happened after this mysterious light whirlwind had swallowed her whole seemed completely erased from her memory. She did not even have a hint as to what had occurred. Could it be that she had dreamed all of this in the end?
