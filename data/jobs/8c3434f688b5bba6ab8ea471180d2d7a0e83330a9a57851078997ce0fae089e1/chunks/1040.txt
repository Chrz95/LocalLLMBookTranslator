Finally, Kaja had her laundry back together again. Already happily singing to herself as she left the room, Laura pulled the blanket off and stood up. With small steps, she went over to her desk and reached for the framed photo that was there. It was an old family photo of Marius and Anna Leander with their two little children in the garden of their house. Laura must have been five years old then. She held Lukas by the hand and grinned at him as if they were both trying to outdo each other, and her parents also smiled happily into the camera. If Laura remembered correctly, this was the last family photo taken before Anna's death. Only a few days later, the terrible accident had occurred.
