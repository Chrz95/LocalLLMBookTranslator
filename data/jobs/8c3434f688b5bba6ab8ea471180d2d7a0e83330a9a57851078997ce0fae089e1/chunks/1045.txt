Morwena hurriedly scrambled to her feet. It was already daylight; the sun was high in the sky, and she had no idea how long she had been unconscious. She also had no clue how she had managed to reach the shore. All she could guess was that Fairy Bride had saved her from drowning after she had been pushed against a rock and fainted. Some way or another, the two-horned creature must have dragged her ashore—wasn’t it washed up there?
