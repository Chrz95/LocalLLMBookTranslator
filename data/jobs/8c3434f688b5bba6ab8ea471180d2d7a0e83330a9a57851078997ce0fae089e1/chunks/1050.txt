Right next to them, a family of wild swuupies was running around wildly—a female with three young ones. With their pink tongues, the young ones eagerly licked water from the river, swuupted joyfully along the shallow bank, and rolled in the sand. When the female noticed the healer, she let out a furious hiss. Then she emitted a warning sound, and instantly the young ones turned around and retreated into the cover of the dense reed bed that lined the riverbank. The mother swuupie growled at Morwena one last time before disappearing into the underbrush.
