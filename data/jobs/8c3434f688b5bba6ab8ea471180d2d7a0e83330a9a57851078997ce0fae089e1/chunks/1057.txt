At breakfast, Laura realized that this would not be her day. First, the milk she had poured over her cornflakes had gone sour. She almost threw up when she put the first spoonful in her mouth. And when she tried to get a new bowl from the kitchen counter, the cornflakes package was empty. The kitchen helper took forever to finally find a new pack, and that's exactly what Kaja had wanted to prevent: Laura had to rush and eat breakfast almost forcefully.
