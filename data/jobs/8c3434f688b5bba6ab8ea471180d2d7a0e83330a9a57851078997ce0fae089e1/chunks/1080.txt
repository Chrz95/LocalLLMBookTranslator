Of course, who else? The scrawny boy with the big horn-rimmed glasses had fiery red hair and more pimples on his face than there were stars in the Milky Way. Laura actually thought it was mean to make fun of others' looks. But that Paul Müller being called "Pimple-Paul" by everyone was okay for her. Given the abundance of his pimples, she found it flattering. Paul Müller could say he was lucky not to be called "Egg-Sack-Paul." That would have been pretty harsh, but not entirely outlandish. He deserved it all right. Not enough that Pimple-Paul was a damn hard worker - in no subject did he do worse than two, except of course for sports. But what was even worse - he was also an utter sycophant and a pest to the Lord. No wonder then that nobody in class liked him. And most teachers didn't either. Only Rebekka Taxus seemed to have fallen head over heels for him. She smiled approvingly at the boy sitting in the front row, and then turned to address the other students.
