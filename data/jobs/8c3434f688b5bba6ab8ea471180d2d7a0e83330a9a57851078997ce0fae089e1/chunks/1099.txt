The scent of Pinky's perfume wafted into Laura's nose. It smelled musty and herbaceous with a distinct musky undertone that she had contemptuously dubbed "Gruftie Scent." Suddenly, Laura remembered: the same musty odor had lingered faintly in the gymnasium the day before. So Rebekka Taxus had been watching Percy and her during their fencing practice—she wondered why?
