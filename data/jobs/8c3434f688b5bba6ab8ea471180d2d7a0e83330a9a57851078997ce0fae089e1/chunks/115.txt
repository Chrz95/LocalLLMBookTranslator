No wonder – after Laura had left the farm of Farmer Dietrich and reached the open field, she had loosened the reins, and Stormwind had taken advantage of the unfamiliar freedom. Just as if he wanted to rid himself with all his might of the confines of the stall, he galloped off at full speed – a stormy, wild ride that Laura had hardly ever experienced before. As the horse hooves pounded like mad, the world seemed to fly past her so quickly that she could barely make out what was happening anymore. The only thing that mattered now were she and her horse. Stormwind's untamed power seemed to be transferred onto Laura, and at that very moment all fear vanished from her. Fear of falling off the horse and getting hurt, fear of failing in school – they were as if blown away. Laura suddenly felt light and carefree, and it seemed as though she was floating.
