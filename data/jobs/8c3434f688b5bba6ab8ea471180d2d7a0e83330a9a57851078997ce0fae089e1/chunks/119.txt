He was still so far away that she could only make out the dark silhouette, which loomed like a threat against the pale sky. But despite the great distance, something unsettling emanated from him, and Laura felt it would be better not to encounter him. An icy chill ran up her face and crept under her anorak, making her shiver. At that very moment, a thick cloud covered the faint sun, and it grew darker. Much darker – as if someone had suddenly dimmed the light. Then, out of nowhere, Laura heard a sinister sound above her.
