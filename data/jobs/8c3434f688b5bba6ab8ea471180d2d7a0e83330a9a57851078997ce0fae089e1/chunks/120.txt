She looked up and saw the crows. There had to be hundreds, maybe even thousands! A huge cloud of black bird bodies was moving across the sky and was quickly approaching. With a hoarse cawing, the flock swirled towards Laura. Suddenly, she noticed that their sounds sounded quite strange. The birds didn't sound like real crows at all; instead, they cawed shrilly and distortedly and almost unrealistically – just as if they came from another world.
