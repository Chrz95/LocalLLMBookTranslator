The Tower Room was located on the topmost floor of the keep and occupied an entire floor. The windows, which offered a view in all four cardinal directions, provided a magnificent vista over Castle Ravenstein and its surroundings. On clear days, one could see for kilometers and sometimes even spot the snow-capped peaks in the distant south. However, today the sky was gray and overcast, and a faint mist obscured visibility. This was not particularly problematic because Miss Mary and Laura had climbed to this room not for the view, which was usually used by various committees of the internate such as the dance group, the guitar ensemble, and also the theater group that Miss Mary led. Laura herself belonged to the troupe of actors, and thus the teacher decided that Laura's lesson in telepathy should take place in this remote room. On one hand, they were undisturbed there, and on the other hand, it would not seem suspicious if the two retreated there for practice.
