Mary Morgain taught English and French at Ravenstein. She was one of the most popular teachers at the boarding school because she knew how to turn lessons from a burdensome duty into a great pleasure. Even Dschingis, the excellent subject teacher, could not keep up with her in this regard. Moreover, Mary hardly ever said a bad word and went out of her way to treat each of her students fairly. This was probably why even the laziest and most rebellious Ravensteiners transformed into near-perfect pupils under her guidance. Even Max Stinkefurz was gentle as a lamb with her, and in contrast to many other classes where he couldn't hold himself back and usually lived up to his nickname, only rarely did it need airing after English and French lessons.

Miss Mary was deeply loved by most of her students at Ravenstein, something very few teachers could claim. And certainly not: Rebekka Taxus. But thankfully, Laura had now discovered the reason for this.
