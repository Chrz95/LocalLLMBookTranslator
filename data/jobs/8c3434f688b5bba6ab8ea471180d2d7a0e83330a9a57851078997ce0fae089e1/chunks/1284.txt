The teacher made a sad face. "Already wrong again, Laura!" she said. "You have not the slightest reason to reproach yourself! You are right - at least almost: Not thinking is much harder than one thinks. But it works! You just need to practice. Often and persistently practicing. Over and over. And you will see: With each time it will become easier for you. Until finally, one day, you reach your goal. And then you will wonder what was actually so difficult about that. Because suddenly it no longer causes you the slightest trouble. And that's why, Laura, close your eyes again and just keep going. Keep going further. Please!"
