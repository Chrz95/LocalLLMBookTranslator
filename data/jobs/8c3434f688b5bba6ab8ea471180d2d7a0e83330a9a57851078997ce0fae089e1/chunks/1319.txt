The small island in the middle of Druden Lake was densely covered with vegetation. However, the flora consisted almost entirely of impenetrable thorny undergrowth interspersed with stinging nettles and poisonous plants. The intern students at Ravenstein were therefore warned emphatically against entering the island upon their admission to the school. Despite this, there were always brave souls who were not deterred by these warnings and secretly attempted to explore the island. These excursions usually yielded little more than scratches, scrapes, and intense itching and rashes if one came into contact with Hercules' wort, milk thistle, or stinking juniper.

Three years ago, however, a particularly severe incident had occurred: A student reported after a nighttime excursion to the island about strange light phenomena and shadowy figures he claimed to have seen there. Immediately afterward, he fell into a feverish delirium and eventually went blind. Fortunately, he was completely healed. But since then, the island has been almost fearfully avoided. That's why Kaja was right: The island in Druden Lake also came across as an excellent hiding spot for the chalice.
