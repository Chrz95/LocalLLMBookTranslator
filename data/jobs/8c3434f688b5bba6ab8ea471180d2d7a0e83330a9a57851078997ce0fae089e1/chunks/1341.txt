The fire crackled in the fireplace, and the flickering light illuminated the professor's face. Aurelius Morgenstern looked tired. Wrapped in his antiquated cloak, he sat at the large round table of his living room and stared ahead. He seemed to be oblivious to everything around him. Neither the light from the hearth fire in the center of the table, nor the candles on the narrow dresser against the wall, nor Laura, who had taken a seat opposite him.
