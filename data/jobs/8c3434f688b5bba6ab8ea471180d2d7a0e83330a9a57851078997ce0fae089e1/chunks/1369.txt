The girl swallowed hard. She couldn't do this. Never! But then Laura tried anyway. She looked at the ball in front of her. Now move already, thought Laura, now come on! But nothing happened. The white wooden ball remained where it was as if glued to its place. How could that even work? But - maybe there was also a trick involved? A trick like one in a magic trick that Aurelius Morgenstern had yet to reveal?
