Once again, he turned his gaze to the white wooden ball that lay on the table before Laura. The girl had the impression that her pupils were slightly constricted. He fixed his eyes on the wooden sphere with such intensity as if he wanted to look through it. After only a few moments, the ball was once more seized by tremors. But what then happened made Laura involuntarily hold her breath: The ball detached from the tabletop and began to rise slowly into the air! Higher she rose and higher until she hovered in mid-air.
