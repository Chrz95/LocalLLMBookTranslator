Uncertainty had settled over Aventerra. An icy wind howled across the highlands of Calderan, whipping the mighty trees in the ancient river forests until they groaned and moaned. It made the silvery whispering grass on the vast plain tremble and drove thick-bellied clouds like fleeing warhorses across the red sky. The two moons of Aventerra, the sulfur-yellow Goldmoon and the shining blue Manystar, barely managed to illuminate the oldest of the ancient planets with their dim light.
