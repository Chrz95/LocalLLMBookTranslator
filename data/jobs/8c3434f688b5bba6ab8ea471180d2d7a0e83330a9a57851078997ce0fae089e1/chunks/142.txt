As Aventerra had existed since the beginning of the worlds, so too did Hellunyat. No one could remember when the fortress was built, and everyone was certain that it would survive even until the end of time. After all, Hellunyat was the home of the Guardian of Light and his followers. Now however, a silence fell over Hellunyat and its inhabitants. Everyone slept, but only those on watch duty at the four towers fought against the onset of sleep.
