For a moment, Paravain was paralyzed with shock, and Alienor could hardly believe what she had just witnessed. But then both rushed to the window and looked down into the courtyard of the castle, where the shattered body of the strange woman must lie—yet there was nothing but a dark shadow scurrying across the ground. Paravain and Alienor raised their eyes to the sky—and saw a powerful winged creature with the face and upper body of an ugly old hag and the wings and feathers of a massive eagle: a harpy!
