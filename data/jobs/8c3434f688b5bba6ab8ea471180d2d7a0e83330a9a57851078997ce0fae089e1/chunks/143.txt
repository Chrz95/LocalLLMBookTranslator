Tarkan, a young knight, and the old Marun served their duty on the East Tower. The tall Tarkan had completed his training in the last moon and was accepted into the circle of knights by the Guardian of Light. It was only his second night watch, and so the young man wandered restlessly back and forth. He strained to peer through the loopholes of the tower's crown out onto the plain. But wherever he looked—eastward, where in the distance lay the treacherous Modermoor behind a narrow strip of oak forest; southward, where the rugged Drachenberges bordered the plain; or northward, where the flat land dropped steeply into the dark Dusky Gorge—all was peaceful and still. Only the wind howled. But to the west lay the dense Raunewald, and the walls of the castle hid the watchman's view.
