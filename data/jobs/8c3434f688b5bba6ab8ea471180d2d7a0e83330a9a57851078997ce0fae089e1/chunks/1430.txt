The next day it began to rain. Fat clouds hung over the sky and released their heavy load of moisture. Burg Ravenstein was enveloped in a damp veil. The following day did not improve matters; rather, an afternoon storm arrived, and the rain intensified. Bitter winds whipped thick rain streaks across the air, so that anyone rash enough to venture outside received merciless and unbridled forceful blows on their face. The rain was so heavy that even the best waterproof clothing offered no protection.
