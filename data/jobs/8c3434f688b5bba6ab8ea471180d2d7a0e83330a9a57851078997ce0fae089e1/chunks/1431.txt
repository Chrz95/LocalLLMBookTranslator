The ground was soon completely saturated and soaked with moisture to the point that it could no longer absorb a single drop of water. The brooks, which usually trickled through the valleys of the hilly landscape and only whispered softly to themselves, had transformed into raging streams whose gurgling sounds were heard far and wide. They overflowed their banks and flooded the land. Even the mirror-like surface of Druden Lake, where two of these wild waters emptied out, rose significantly. The storm lasted for three full days. It was not until the third night that the rain finally ceased.
