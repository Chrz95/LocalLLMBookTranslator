Her telekinetic skills, however, made little progress. The lessons with Professor Morgenstern were quite exhausting. The sick man had to take more and shorter breaks increasingly often as he grew weaker day by day. He constantly lost consciousness. Miss Mary advised him to be careful and rather skip the classes out of consideration for his weakened health. But that was something the professor would not hear of. After all, he was the only guardian who knew about telekinesis. And if Laura didn't learn from him, then whom should she learn from?
