The cold seeped into Tarkan's bones. As he drew his fur-lined cloak closer over the leather breastplate, an unfamiliar sound startled him. Tarkan listened intently. And indeed—there it was again, this eerie whistling. He had already placed a hand on the hilt of his sword when he realized what that ghastly noise meant: It was merely a harmless night whistler, whose mournful call echoed in the distance. Shaking his head slightly in annoyance at himself, Tarkan continued on his way.
