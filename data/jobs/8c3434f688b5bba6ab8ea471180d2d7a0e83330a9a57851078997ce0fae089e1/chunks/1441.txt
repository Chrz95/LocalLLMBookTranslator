With all the various occupations, Laura had barely managed to find time for studying for the math test. Yet it was incredibly important. If she were to write another bad grade again, the risk of not achieving the class goal would increase once more. Rebekka Taxus would definitely not choose easy tasks for the test in order to make things easier for Laura. On the contrary: The Taxus would set particularly difficult tasks because if Laura failed at the end of the year, she would be forced to leave the internate, and that would mean there would be one less guard on Ravenstein.
