Marun smiled quietly to himself. For the underweight man, the guard duty over the years had long become a routine. The old knight could hardly remember how many of these tedious, endlessly stretching hours he already had behind him. At least he had learned one thing: endless waiting and staring into twilight or darkness could play tricks on you. Even harmless sounds and shadows could seem dangerous in tension. But Marun knew that calmness and serenity were the best defense against such illusions and false images. He sat down, leaned his back against the wall, folded his hands over his round belly, and dozed off drowsily. Now he raised his head and glared at Tarkan angrily.
