Only then did Laura understand what Percy meant by "Tunnel" – some also called it the "Zone" or "Flash." Runners referred to this almost trance-like state that they occasionally entered during long and exhausting runs when they overcame the feeling of apparent exhaustion and simply kept running further. Many felt as if they were in a trance, completely detached from earthly gravity. Others said they felt like they were floating and experienced an almost euphoric sensation – which is why they constantly tried to enter this tunnel again.
