A short hour later, the three friends stood on the boat dock near the bathing bay. The wooden planks were black with moisture and swayed slightly. The sky over Druden Lake looked like freshly poured lead. A strong wind swept across the water surface and gave it a restless ripple pattern. Two rowboats were tied to the dock. They danced restlessly up and down and tugged at their ropes. It almost seemed as if they couldn't wait to finally be led out onto the lake.
