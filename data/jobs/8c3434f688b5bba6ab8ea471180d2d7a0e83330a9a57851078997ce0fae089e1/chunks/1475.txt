Laura nodded. "I'm sorry, Kaja. But since the accident—" She broke off because the memory of that terrible day suddenly became so vivid again as if it had happened just yesterday. Immediately she felt the boundless fear that had gripped her then. She saw the gurgling water again in front of her, which flooded into the car and rose rapidly higher and higher. And she saw her mother, who was trapped behind the steering wheel and desperately trying to undo her seatbelt.
