But Anna Leander had not succeeded. She only managed to free Laura from the safety belt and push her out of the sinking car at the last moment. Laura saw it again in front of her: the shore towards which she swam with all her might. Laura still remembered that she had turned her head back, trying to look behind her... But what happened afterwards, she could no longer recall. Apparently, memory had taken pity on her and completely erased those terrifying images. Although this was now more than eight years ago, a strange feeling of oppression settled over Laura's chest, and breathing suddenly became difficult for her.
