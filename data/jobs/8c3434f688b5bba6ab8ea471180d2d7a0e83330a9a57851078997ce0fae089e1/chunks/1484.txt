Kaja shrugged. "If you think so," she said and turned to the boat with a tortured smile. Lukas pulled it right up next to the pier. Despite that, the boat swayed violently as Kaja climbed aboard. She let out a sharp cry and lost her balance. It was only thanks to Lukas' lightning-fast reaction that she didn't fall headfirst into the frigid water - his hand shot forward just in time, grabbed her by the collar of her anorak, and held her steady.
