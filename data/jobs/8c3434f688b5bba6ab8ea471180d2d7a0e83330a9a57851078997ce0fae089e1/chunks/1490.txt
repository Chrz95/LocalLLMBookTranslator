Laura bentled down, untied the knot, threw the rope to Lukas, and gave the boat a little push. Instantly, it slid out onto the lake. The restless water made it rock, and Kaja's gaze grew even more anxious. With a few precise strokes, Lukas righted the boat so that its tip pointed towards the island. Then he began pulling on the oars through the water. He pulled with strength and regularity as if he had never done anything else in his life, and they glided calmly towards the island.
