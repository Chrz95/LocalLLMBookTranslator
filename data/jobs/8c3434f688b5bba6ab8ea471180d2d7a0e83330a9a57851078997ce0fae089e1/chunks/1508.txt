A few minutes later, the boat had reached the island. Lukas rowed close to the shore in search of a suitable spot for anchoring. However, this turned out to be extremely difficult, almost impossible. A repellent fringe of prickly bramble bushes, whitethorn, and blackthorn bushes encircled the island. They stood so closely together that passage seemed nearly impossible, and thus they had to practically circle the entire island until at last they found a small cove whose shoreline was less densely overgrown.
