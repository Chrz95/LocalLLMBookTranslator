Kaja initially did not understand what Lukas meant, but then she saw what Lukas was wondering about: only a few meters away from them, a strange climbing plant was winding around a slender birch tree. Kaja had never seen such a plant before. It moved like a green snake around the tree. The large fleshy leaves were almost unrealistically green. But most strikingly were the abundant flower buds of the plant. They were a strikingly robust red and had long yellow stamens.
