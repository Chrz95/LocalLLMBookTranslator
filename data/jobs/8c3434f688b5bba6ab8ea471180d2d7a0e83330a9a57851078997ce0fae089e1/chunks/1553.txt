The Fairy Bride also suffered under the unbearable heat. The Two-Horned creature struggled through the deep desert sand, and Morwena almost felt her mount's exhaustion in her own body as she leaned into the stirrup and peered toward the horizon - although her vision was obscured by a powerful dust cloud, she thought she could make out the towering rock pinnacle in the shimmering distance that marked the beginning of the Dark Gorge. Morwena gathered new courage and spurred Fairy Bride with gentle urging. Suddenly, she spotted the wandering sand vortex.
