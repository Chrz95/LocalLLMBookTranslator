Tarkan hesitated. He did not know exactly what he should do. The Guardian of Light had entrusted his life to the knights - and Knight Paravain, the first among the thirteen White Knights and thus the leader of the bodyguard, had recently sworn the young knights at swordplay to remain vigilant in their fight against the forces of darkness. For since time immemorial, the dark powers had been at war with the powers of light and were constantly striving to conquer the good and bring about the rule of eternal nothingness.
