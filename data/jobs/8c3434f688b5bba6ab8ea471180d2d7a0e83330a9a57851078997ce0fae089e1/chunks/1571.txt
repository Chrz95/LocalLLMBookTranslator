Despite the early afternoon hour, twilight already hung over Henkerswald. The friends felt uneasy as they followed the narrow path towards the crypt. Kaja looked around nervously, and Laura and Lukas' faces had taken on a tense expression too. The sound of their footsteps was muffled by an old carpet of pine needles and decaying leaves. The smell of decay and rot hung in the air. Again and again, rustling and whispering could be heard in the undergrowth, and crackling and snapping sounds were also audible. Yet no one could be seen causing these noises. Not a soul or beast.
