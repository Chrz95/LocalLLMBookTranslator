In earlier times, a clearing in the forest had served as an execution site. Many innocent people were among those unfortunate souls who met their end at this gruesome place by the hangman's hand - especially Reimar von Ravenstein. A threadbare piece of evidence or the testimony of a gossipy witness was enough to seal one's fate, and soon it was over for that poor soul or that poor woman. And if the Cruel Knight had taken a particular interest in someone, then even the hangman got work done without a court first pronouncing a dishonorable verdict.
