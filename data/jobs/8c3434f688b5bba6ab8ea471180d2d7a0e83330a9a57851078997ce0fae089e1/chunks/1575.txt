Since those days, people in the area had been telling each other various spooky tales about the ghosts of these innocent victims, who were supposedly restless and bent on revenge, prowling around aimlessly in the Hangman's Woods. Naturally, these ghost stories were also enthusiastically passed along at school. No one had actually seen these ghosts yet, but a few Ravensteiners claimed to have heard their eerie, bone-chilling screams. Or at least they knew someone who had heard them. Of course, Laura was aware of these rumors too, but she never took them seriously.
