A short while later, the walls of the Old Crypt shimmered through the trees. From a distance, the half-ruined edifice already seemed intimidating. Laura dimly recalled that she had seen a similar ruin not too long ago on an old painting. Sayelle had dragged her and Lukas to the museum where it hung. It was the only one among countless paintings there that Laura had found truly impressive. For the dilapidated tomb in the painting had really seemed eerie - almost as eerie as this crypt in Henkerswald. But she could no longer remember the name of the painter.
