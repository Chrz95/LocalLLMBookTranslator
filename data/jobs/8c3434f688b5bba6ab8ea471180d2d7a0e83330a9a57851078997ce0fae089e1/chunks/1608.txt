She took another step towards the grave to get to the bottom of things when a sinister rustling filled the air. Surprised, Laura looked up, and at that very moment she felt even colder. The mistletoe bushes in the treetops began to twist! They became alive and transformed into enormous black crows within a few seconds. As if by some secret command, these death birds flew from the branches and let out the same terrifying cawing as the crow flock that had followed Laura during her ride. Then they descended upon their friends. Countless pairs of black wings fell, the flapping of the wings was clearly audible, and the screeching grew louder and sharper. Already a dense cloud of black feathers circled above the intruders, who pressed together and involuntarily drew in their heads. The hoarse cawing of the crows hurt her ears, and the fear of being attacked by those sharp beaks made her friends' blood freeze in their veins.
