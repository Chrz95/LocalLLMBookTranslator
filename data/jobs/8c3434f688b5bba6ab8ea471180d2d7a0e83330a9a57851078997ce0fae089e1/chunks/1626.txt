As blood pounded wildly through Laura's veins, she finally realized who was before her: it was Albin Ellerking, the gardener, and perched on his shoulder was Groll, his cat. In the darkness, he appeared to Laura like an eerie gremlin. Groll had raised his bushy tail, hunched over, and let out a hoarse growl. The yellow slit eye in that ugly feline face glinted.
