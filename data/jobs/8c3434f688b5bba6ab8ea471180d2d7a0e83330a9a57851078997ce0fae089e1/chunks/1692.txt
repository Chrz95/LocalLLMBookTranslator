Closely huddled together, they crouched behind the trunk of a mighty old oak tree in the park. Their eyes were fixed on a small house made of gray fieldstones that stood not far from them in the shade of a beech tree and was partially hidden by a large hazelnut bush. There probably weren't more than two or three rooms inside it. On one side of the little house, there were two tiny windows visible. Faint light filtered through the sprossen glass with its pretty curtains. On the other side, there was also a window and in addition, a low wooden door. The crooked roof was covered with slate tiles. Smoke curled from the chimney into the darkening sky.
