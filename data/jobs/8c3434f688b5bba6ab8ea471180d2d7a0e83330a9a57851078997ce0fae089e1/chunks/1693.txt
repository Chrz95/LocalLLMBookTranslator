In the times of Reimar von Ravenstein, the castle chaplain had lived in that house. However, not for very long, because when the devout servant of God dared to make complaints about his lord's somewhat ungodly lifestyle, the executioner already had to return to his gruesome trade on the following day. Over the centuries, the building had seen many different inhabitants, but now Attila Morduk lived there endlessly, the housemaster of the internate.
