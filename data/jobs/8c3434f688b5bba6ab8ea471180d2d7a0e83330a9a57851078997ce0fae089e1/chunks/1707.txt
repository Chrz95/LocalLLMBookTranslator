The caretaker seemed not to notice that he was being watched by three pairs of eyes. With exaggerated strides, he hurried along the path that wound through the trees in the park and led from his hidden cottage to the castle building. His long arms flailed awkwardly at his sides as his bulky body moved in a swaying sailor's gait. Suddenly, he began to whistle a tune. Laura couldn't believe her ears - the dark, grim Attila was whistling a cheerful melody. A few moments later, the caretaker was swallowed up by the darkness.
