After Laura had closed the door again, she saw - nothing. She stood completely in darkness. However, she did not dare to turn on the light. The risk of being noticed by someone was simply too great. It was warm inside the hut, almost stifling, and a strange smell hit Laura. She sniffed but could not identify it. There was a faint musty odor with a slightly putrid undertone. But there was still something that Laura couldn't quite put her finger on. It smelled like... like...
