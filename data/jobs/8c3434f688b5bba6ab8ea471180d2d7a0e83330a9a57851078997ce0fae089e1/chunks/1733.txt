The container with the aggressive cobra stood on a large shelf that occupied the entire length of the room's wall. Countless terrariums were lined up within it, housing other creatures as well. Mostly reptiles. But Laura also spotted spiders and scorpions, whose sight did not scare her. Yet, an oppressive feeling crept over her. Attila Morduk must be a peculiar bird if he felt comfortable in the company of such disgusting creatures. But somehow, it suited him.
