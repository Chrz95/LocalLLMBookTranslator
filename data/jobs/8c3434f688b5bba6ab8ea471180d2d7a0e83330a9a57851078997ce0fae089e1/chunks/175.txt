As Dietrich stepped out onto the yard, dusk had already set in. The cold winter air stung his nose. He flared his nostrils and sniffed once, twice like an animal taking in scents. Then he looked up at the sky, where dark clouds were gathering. There would be a storm that night. Kastor Dietrich turned around and saw through the open stable door directly into Sturmwind's stall, where Laura was just pitching hay into the manger.
