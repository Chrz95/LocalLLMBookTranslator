The library key! Which was that damned library key? Although all the keys were adorned with a pendant, their inscription was so archaic that Laura could hardly decipher it. Her Uroma, her father's long-dead grandmother, had written in the same peculiar handwriting, recalled Laura. Marius Leander had discovered some sheets of paper in the basement of her house containing her own self-written stories. But when Laura tried to read them, she found the strange script so difficult that she soon gave up on the idea.
