Laura quickly took the large metal key and slipped it into her anorak pocket. She turned around and was about to scurry towards the door when two orange-red points suddenly glowed from the darkness, like a pair of giant fireflies, slowly flying towards her. Laura was startled - what could that be? Only when the eerie light duo was already close to her face did she realize: it was eyes – the eyes of an enormous snake! A boa. Laura froze in place. The reptile stared at her hypnotically. Laura wanted to back away but couldn't tear her gaze from those glowing points. She was paralyzed. She knew she had to move, that she needed to flee quickly if she didn’t want to be ensnared by the boa's deadly embrace. But she couldn't. The boa’s gaze held her in place and seemed to hold her with invisible forces.
