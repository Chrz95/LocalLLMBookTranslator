Dick and dark, he wove between the trees and rolled out onto the plain. Others followed, countless, enormous black clouds of mist rose suddenly like a dark army - an army that grew ceaselessly. The mists whispered to each other. Whispering mists were nothing unusual on Aventerra; they existed – like singing winds and dancing shadows – since time immemorial. But with these whispering mists here, there was something special, for in them was life, even if it was more to be sensed than seen.
