The ceiling light flickered on. The sudden brightness blinded Laura, and she squinted for a moment. Then she saw the little cabinet. A few steps from the side table it stood against the wall, just under five feet tall with two narrow woven doors. It was Fräulein Amalie Bröselsam's cabinet, the librarian of Ravenstein. She liked to hang her jacket or coat in there and place her hat inside as well. Additionally, she stored all sorts of personal oddities in it.
