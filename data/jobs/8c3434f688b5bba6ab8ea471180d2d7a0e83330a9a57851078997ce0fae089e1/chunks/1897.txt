Laura's heart hammered to the point of bursting, and she gasped in horror. Quickly, she placed her hand over her mouth so as not to give herself away with her heavy breathing. At the same time, the tickling in her nose grew stronger, almost unbearable. Laura wrinkled her nose to suppress the urge to sneeze, but it was no use. On the contrary: The tingling became unbearably intense. She was lost. With thumb and forefinger, she pinched her nose shut and held her breath.
