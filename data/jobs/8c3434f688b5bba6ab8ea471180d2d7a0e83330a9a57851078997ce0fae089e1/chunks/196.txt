The knight wanted to let out a loud warning cry, but the scream got stuck in his throat. He only managed to choke out strangled sounds as an eerie power seemed to rob him of his breath. Tarkan gagged and struggled desperately for air. The sword slipped from his powerless hands and crashed to the ground with a loud thud when a terrifyingly large cloud of mist swirled over the tower's roof. Borboron, the Black Prince himself, rose before Tarkan! When the boy saw the piercing red eyes that mercilessly stared at him, it was too late. A mighty sword emerged from the mist and plunged deep into Tarkan's throat. The young knight fell to his knees, a gush of blood and a moan escaping his mouth.
