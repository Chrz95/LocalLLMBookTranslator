Alienor hesitated with her answer and watched the squire who sat beside her on a bench in the garden of the hospital. Surrounded by high stone walls, the Grail Castle lay directly behind the ward, far removed from the bustling activity of the castle grounds. The healers cultivated and tended to the plants here from which they harvested the herbs, ointments, and tinctures for the care of the sick. The sounds of the blacksmith's hammering, the calls of animals in the stables, and the cries of servants drifted over from the courtyard. Although Hellunyat was threatened by its downfall, everyday life continued as usual.
