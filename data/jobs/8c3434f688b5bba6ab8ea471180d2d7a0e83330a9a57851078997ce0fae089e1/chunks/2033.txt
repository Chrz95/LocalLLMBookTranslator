Aura and Percy sat on the backs of their horses and galloped across the fields and meadows of the hilly countryside. Percy's Salamar had the same untamed energy as Laura's Stormwind. Hardly did the riders let go of the reins when both stallions fell into a wild gallop. It seemed they were trying to decide who was faster between them. Their leaps grew longer, and their pace became more rapid. Finally, it was so wild that Percy had to intervene.
