It was obvious that it was a trinket box. On all the strings were strange patterns, which Laura had never seen before. She had stretched out her hand when her father suddenly appeared and rudely demanded she give him the box. Laura was startled and recoiled, but immediately Marius explained in a more conciliatory tone that the contents of the box were indeed intended for her, though she would only receive it at a later time. "You will learn what it is about on the Thirteenth Festival," he had smiled and taken the box from her. Soon after, Laura had forgotten the incident and had never thought about it again since.
