Laura stood in the shadow of a wall. As she looked around, she realized that she was on the courtyard of Ravenstein Castle. The windows of the castle were dark, and nowhere did light shine. It must be quite late. Probably after midnight. The night was clear and frosty. Laura's breath formed small clouds before her mouth. The pale, still almost full moon stood high in the sky and cast everything into a pale light. Laura now understood why she had smelled an intense snow scent in the afternoon instead of Kastor and Nikodemus Dietrich - the whole land was covered with a thick blanket of snow.
