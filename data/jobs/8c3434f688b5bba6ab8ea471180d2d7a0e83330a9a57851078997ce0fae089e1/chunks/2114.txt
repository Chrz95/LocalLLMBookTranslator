Marius Leander darted around the corner of the wall. The narrow path that ran along the length of the school building had been cleared and sprinkled with snow. Marius was about to follow him when he suddenly changed his mind. He stopped abruptly and vanished behind a dense hedge that lined the path, with a quick leap. The footsteps and panting of his pursuers were already audible as Marius suddenly noticed that his foot had left a clearly visible impression in the deep snow beside the path. Quickly, he bent out from behind the hedge and smudged the betraying trace with his hands. Just in time, he pulled himself back into his hiding spot because only moments later, his pursuers burst around the corner as well.
