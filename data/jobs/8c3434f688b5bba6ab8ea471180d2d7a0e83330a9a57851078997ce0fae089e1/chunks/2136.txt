Hands flew to the hilts of the swords, and with drawn weapons, the men charged at Marius, who was helplessly ensnared. He fought fiercely, trying with all his might to escape their grasp, but he had no chance. He had to give in to the overwhelming power. The Black Knights seized him, roughly pulling his hands behind his back and binding them together. Two of the men dragged Marius by his upper arms toward the door. The third followed them with a grim face.
