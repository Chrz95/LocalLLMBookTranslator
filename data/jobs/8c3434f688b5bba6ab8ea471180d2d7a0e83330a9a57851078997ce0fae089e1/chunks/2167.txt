Percy Valiant had to help Laura into the saddle. Without her own strength, she wouldn't have made it. On their ride back to the stable, Laura clung tightly to the saddle horn. Yet she still swayed dangerously from side to side. Even Sturmwind seemed to sense that his rider was severely weakened. The stallion moved carefully, placing each foot with caution and always mindful of avoiding any frantic movements. However, the girl would have surely fallen off if her teacher hadn't ridden close by and occasionally supported her.
