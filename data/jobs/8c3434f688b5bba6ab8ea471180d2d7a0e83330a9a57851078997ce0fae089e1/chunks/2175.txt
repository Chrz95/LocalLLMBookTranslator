She had not even reached the first floor when she already regretted having rejected Percy's help. With each passing minute, she felt weaker and weaker. Every step cost her a superhuman effort. Clutching both hands onto the railing, she pulled herself up step by step. When she finally reached the mezzanine, she had to rest for a moment first. Panting, she let herself sink down on the ground and leaned against the railing with her back.
