Laura breathed heavily and her pulse steadied again. She placed the necklace back into its box, locked it away, and took a few steps toward the photo to examine it more closely. It must have been taken only a few weeks before Anna's death. At that time she was twenty-eight years old, a pretty young woman. Laura found her even prettier than that. She herself had inherited Anna’s blonde hair and blue eyes as well. On the photo, Anna looked thoughtful.
