Maybe she had already sensed what would happen back then, Laura suddenly thought. But at the same moment, she dismissed that idea. How could such a thing be possible? No one, not even anyone, could have foreseen that Anna Leander, several weeks later on her journey from Hohenstadt to Ravenstein, would suddenly need to dodge two large black dogs that had appeared out of nowhere in front of her car, causing her to lose control and land in a lake.
