Paravain grabbed the cup, took a sip and set the carafe back on the dresser. Slowly he walked to the window. He looked out into the darkness that had settled over the vast highlands of Calderan. The sky was cloudless, and nothing obstructed the view of the two moons of Aventerra. The Earth moon shone brightly and blue as always. But the Gold Moon was pale because it had only traveled halfway to its zenith. Seven days would pass until it would again shine in full glory.
