Exactly seven days remained to find the Cup of Enlightenment, a minuscule span of time compared with nearly thirteen moons since their allies had been searching for the cup on the Human Star. All their efforts had been in vain, and a anxious question arose within the young knight: If they hadn't succeeded in finding the cup during this long period, how could he then hope that their search would be successful in these seven days? Wasn't it hopeless and all hoping in vain?
