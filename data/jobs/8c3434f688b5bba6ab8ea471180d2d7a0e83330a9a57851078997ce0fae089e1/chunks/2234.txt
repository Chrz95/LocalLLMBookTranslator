The sound of the door opening made him stop and turn around. Alienor, Morwen's elevin, left the sleeping chamber with a large bowl and moved almost silently towards the exit. As every evening, she was heading to the kitchen to fetch fresh water. Her blond braids swayed gently as she strode quickly through the hall. The girl had nearly reached the door when Paravain called out: "Wait just a moment, Alienor!"
