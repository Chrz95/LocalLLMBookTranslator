It was almost mouse-like silent in the classroom. Wordlessly, the 7th grade sweated over their math test. Only the faint rustling of paper could be heard, the scratching of pencils as they scurried across the test sheets, and the clack of Rebekka Taxus's high heels on the wooden floorboards. The math teacher strolled slowly through rows of school desks. Like an eagle hunting prey, she turned her head from side to side and scanned over the students' faces. Wherever Rebekka looked, only thoughtful and strained expressions were visible. Some already showed signs of panic and desperation.
