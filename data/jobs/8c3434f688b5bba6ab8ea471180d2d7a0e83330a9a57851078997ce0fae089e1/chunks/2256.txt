Hoppel preferred it that way. Pinky Taxus would mercilessly give him a six if she caught him again in the "undercutting attempt," as she liked to call the act of copying. Irritated, he rolled his eyes and turned back to his tasks. At the same time, he swore that next time he would give Pickel-Paule a good thrashing. This miserable slacker had to pay for his selfishness, and he deserved it.
