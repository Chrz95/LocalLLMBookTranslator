Laura noticed the teacher not at all. She stared almost apathetically at the test paper. She felt very tired. Utterly exhausted. Yet she had slept more than twelve hours in the previous night. However, this morning she hadn't heard the alarm clock and Kaja had only managed to rouse her with great difficulty. Laura would have preferred to sleep on. After all, sleeping was what she wanted right now - nothing but sleep. The math assignment could stay stolen from her! When Laura looked at the test paper, she noticed that she still hadn't entered a single solution. How? She had not the slightest idea how to solve those damned problems. She didn't even understand what the hidden questions in the text tasks actually said. Laura's head was completely empty; her brain as if burned out. She felt unable to form even one coherent thought. And of course, she couldn't read Pickel-Paul's thoughts either. Not a single one! However, despite her exhaustion, it was clear to her: If the emptiness in her head persisted and she didn't manage to solve at least one problem by the end of the hour, then she would get zero points - a flat six.
