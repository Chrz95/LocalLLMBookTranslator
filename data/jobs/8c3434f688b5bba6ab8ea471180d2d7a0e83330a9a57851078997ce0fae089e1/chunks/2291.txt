Angry, Laura kicked a stone into the water and wandered restlessly back and forth. Finally, she stopped and stared out at the lake. A gentle breeze blew from the water, and beneath her feet, the waves splashed against the shore and lapped at her boots. A solitary duck flapped heavily over and landed noisily on the water. Over the small island, a translucent veil of mist had settled, whose contours changed slowly in the gentle breeze.
