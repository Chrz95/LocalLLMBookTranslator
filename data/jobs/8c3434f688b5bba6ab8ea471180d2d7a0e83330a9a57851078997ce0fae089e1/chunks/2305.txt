Larik rode out of the shadow of the trees and stopped his steppe pony at the edge of the forest. The brown horse snorted. Although steppe ponies were extremely enduring animals, it seemed he was grateful for this first break that his rider had given him since their departure from Hellunyat. They had ridden all night and half a day. The fear that Paravain might still discover his plan kept Alarik pushing forward without pause. Now, however, having left the Dragon Mountains behind and reached the border of Goldenland, he felt secure.
