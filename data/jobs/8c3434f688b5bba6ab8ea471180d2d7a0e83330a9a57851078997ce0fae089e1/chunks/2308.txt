Goldenland could still impressively demonstrate why it bore its name: swaying fields where the crops ripened, lush meadows in verdant green, and dense forests with mighty trees whose treetops seemed to touch the sky, bathed in golden sunlight that cast glittering light reflections on the broad river that flowed lazily through the valley's middle. But Alarik knew that this sight was deceptive. When Elysion entered the Eternal Darkness, these Black Clouds would also spread here and the Eternal Nothingness would devour his homeland as well. Alarik was glad that at least in Goldenland it seemed clear that everything was still in order and nothing of the impending disaster could be seen.
