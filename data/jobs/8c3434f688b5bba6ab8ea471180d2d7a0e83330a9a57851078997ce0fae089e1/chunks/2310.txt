At the thought of his family, Alaric suddenly felt a pang in his heart. Since he and Alienor had left their father's castle two years ago to follow Elysion's call to the Grail Castle, neither had he set foot on his homeland nor seen his beloved ones again. Full of longing, his gaze clung to the castle, and almost uncontrollably, Alaric yearned for a visit with his parents and siblings, however brief it might be. But there was no time for that.
