His goal lay further south, beyond the sulfur pool that had to be located at the edge of the horizon. Alarik squinted his eyes shut but could not see any trace of the Dark Fortress. He also couldn't see the swamp itself. Instead, there was a cloud of boiling steam and murky yellow mist hovering over the land where he suspected it would be. It was impenetrable by sight, but Alarik knew what this brew meant: it consisted of poisonous sulfur fumes that were continuously emitted from the swamp to form an asphyxiating mask over everything that dared approach too closely.
