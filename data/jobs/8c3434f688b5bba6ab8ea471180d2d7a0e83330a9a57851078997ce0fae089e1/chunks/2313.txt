Not that he had not been convinced of the correctness of his plan. On the contrary: During the night ride, he had thought about it again and again, and he had come to the conclusion that it was better to act than simply to wait. But Alienor was right: The undertaking was dangerous. Life-threatening even. Especially since he did not know the path through the swamp as well as he had claimed to his sister. He had only wanted to calm Alienor down. In truth, Silvan had told her about the secret path just once. And that was so long ago that he hardly remembered it anymore. But he had to try anyway.
