Alarik was about to set off when he heard the Grolff's cry. The piercing scream came directly from the forest behind him. The boy had never seen one of these eerie creatures, which the inhabitants of the Güldenland only dared to speak of in hushed tones. Alarik even suspected that no one had ever actually seen a Grolff before. Neither his father, who was a wise man, nor his mother, nor anyone else could describe this creature that supposedly wreaked havoc in the border forests. Yet it was agreed upon in Güldenland what these cries meant. Alarik's grandmother had told him when he was still a child: "Whoever hears the cry of a Grolff is doomed to misfortune," she had whispered. Great misfortune—and sometimes even death! And Alarik had believed every word she said.
