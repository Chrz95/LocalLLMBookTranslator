Miss Amalie Bröselsam looked up from a book, pushed her reading glasses onto the tip of her nose, peered over the round lenses, and glared at Laura with a suspicious chicken-like look. The girl stood near the lending counter and had been studying the thick volumes on the shelf in front of him for more than five minutes now, seemingly with the utmost interest. A large sign indicated that this section was dedicated to "Ancient Byzantine Art History," which exactly matched what Miss Bröselsam's suspicion had been about.
