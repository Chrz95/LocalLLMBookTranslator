He lowered his gaze to the sheet and read: "A very special role is played by the number thirteen in the mythological time reckoning. After that, the year begins and ends on the day of the winter solstice; the time between these two points is divided into thirteen months, each consisting of twenty-eight days. For this reason, thirteen is considered a sacred number in many cultures. However, people whose birthday falls on the thirteenth day of the thirteenth month are said to possess very special powers and abilities – because they were born under the sign of thirteen."
