Laura gasped in exertion. She had her fingertips of the right hand wedged into the narrow gap between the wall and the skirting board and was pulling it back and forth with great force. Her fingers hurt, and the nail on her middle finger was broken off. Some fingers were already split open, leaving bloody smears on the light-colored plaster. The girl pulled, tugged, and yanked, but the skirting board did not budge from its place.
