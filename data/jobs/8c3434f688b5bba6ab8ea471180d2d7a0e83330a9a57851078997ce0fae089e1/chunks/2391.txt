The translucent sphere had the size of a child's head and shone in the glow of the fire. Inside it bubbled an impenetrable cloud of black mist. The mist twisted spirally, faster and faster, until it suddenly cleared. When it dissipated, it revealed a miniature landscape. Syrin’s head shot forward; his eyes glowed feverishly, and his thin lips curled into a sneering grin as he pointed to the magical stone. "This fool! Does he really think we can ignore him?"
