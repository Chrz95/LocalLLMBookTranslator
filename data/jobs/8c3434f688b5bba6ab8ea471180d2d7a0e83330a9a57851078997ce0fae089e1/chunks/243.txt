Lukas saw his sister over the rim of his large glasses, which had slipped down to the tip of his nose. But Laura only frowned in annoyance. She could really not stand it when her brother acted like he knew everything. Of course, he knew more than she did. That was no wonder! He kept his head stuck in some clever books or searched the internet for the latest scientific discoveries. Let him do that if he wanted to! She had nothing against it as long as he didn't put on airs and thought himself better than her.
