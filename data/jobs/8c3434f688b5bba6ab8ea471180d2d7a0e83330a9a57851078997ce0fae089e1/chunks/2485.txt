When Magda left the room, Laura began to tell her story. She initiated her into the great secret that holds our world together and connects Earth and Aventerra, the oldest of the ancient planets. She spoke of the Guardian of Light and the Prince of Darkness; of the Warriors of Light and the Masters of Darkness; of the Watchers and the Dark Ones; of the eternal struggle between Good and Evil; and about her special task that had been assigned to her.
