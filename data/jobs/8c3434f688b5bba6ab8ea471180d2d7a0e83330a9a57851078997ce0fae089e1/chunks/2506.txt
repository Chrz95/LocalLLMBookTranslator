The deep darkness had settled over Burg Ravenstein even though it was only just past four o'clock in the afternoon. Although there was still no snow on the ground, one could clearly feel that winter was tightening its grip on the land more and more. It was very cold; temperatures fluctuated around freezing point, and the air felt unpleasantly humid. A thick fog crept around the buildings of the castle, and through the park, dense clouds of mist drifted by. The gray soup enveloped trees and bushes so that they could only be recognized as shadows. Suddenly, between them appeared a blurred red spot that slowly moved.
