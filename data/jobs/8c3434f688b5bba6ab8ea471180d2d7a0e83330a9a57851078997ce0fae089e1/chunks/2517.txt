Only the ticking of the clock could be heard in the room; otherwise it was silent. Kaja sat silently on the bed and stared at Laura with disbelief. It seemed as though she couldn't grasp what her friend had just told her about Lukas. She shook her head repeatedly, as if to convince herself that she wasn't dreaming. Finally, she took a chocolate bar from the desk drawer, broke off a piece, and thoughtfully put it in her mouth.
