The statue of the Stone Rider emerged from the darkness. Again, Laura felt the oppressive feeling that gripped her every time she saw him. She stopped and allowed the beam of her flashlight to slowly sweep over the monument. The bright light beam crept up the gray horse, glided over the imposing rider's figure, and lingered on the granite face of the rider. Then Reimar von Ravenstein narrowed his left eye and winked menacingly at Laura.
