In the vast throne room of Hellunyat, there was silence. Only the smoldering glow from the sooty torches hanging on the wrought-iron wall brackets and the crackling sound of the fire in the stone fireplace could be heard. Elysion, the Guardian of Light, sat in a chair and stared absentmindedly at the flames. The flickering light from the fire cast an ominous glow across his fearsome face, and the pungent smell of resinous wood smoke filled his nose.
