They had reached the moat and stepped onto the narrow wooden bridge that stretched across it. The planks creaked as they crossed over. Laura leaned over the railing of the bridge and shone her light down into the moat, which was about four to five meters deep here. A musty odor rose from below. Dense foliage lay on the ground in the moat. The autumn storms had blown it there, where it now slowly decayed. Suddenly, something red lit up in the beam of light.
