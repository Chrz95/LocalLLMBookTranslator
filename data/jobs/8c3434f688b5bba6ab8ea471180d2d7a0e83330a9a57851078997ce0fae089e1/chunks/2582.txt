The moat of the castle fell almost vertically down. Clinging to bushes and roots, they struggled their way down. Halfway up, Laura suddenly lost her grip and slid down. She screamed out, her fingers desperately searching for something to hold onto. But they reached into nothingness, and Laura slid unimpeded down the slope. Fortunately, her fall was cushioned by the leaves, so she only came away with minor scratches on her hands.
