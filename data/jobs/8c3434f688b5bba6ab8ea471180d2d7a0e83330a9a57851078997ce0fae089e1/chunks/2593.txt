A bandage was wrapped around Magda's head, and a man in a red-and-yellow fleece jacket walked beside the stretcher. "Paramedic" stood in large yellow illuminated letters on his back. In his hand he held a transparent plastic bottle from which liquid dripped into Magda's arm via a tube. The paramedics pushed the stretcher into the ambulance, and the doctor climbed inside as well. He hung the drip on a hook, bent over Magda, pulled her eyelids back, and shone a flashlight in her eyes. Nothing more could be seen because the paramedics closed the rear doors and got into the driver's cab. Moments later, the sirens wailed, and the ambulance sped away.
