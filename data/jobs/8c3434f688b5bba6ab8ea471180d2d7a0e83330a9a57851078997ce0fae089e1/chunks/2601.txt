Only Laura, Lukas, and Kaja stood with Percy Valiant still. The two had been shaken by what had happened to Magda. Despite all the misfortune, Magda had apparently been lucky. At least that was Percy's opinion. He was convinced that her fall would have been fatal if not for the thick layer of foliage on the ground of the moat. On a harder surface, Magda would certainly have broken her neck.
