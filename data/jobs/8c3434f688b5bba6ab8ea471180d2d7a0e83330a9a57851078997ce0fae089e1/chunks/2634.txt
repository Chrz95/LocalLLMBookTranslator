The Taxus retreated. Although she had already seen the flicker of red anger in Quintus' eyes countless times and that fiery gleam only lasted for fractions of a second each time, it never failed to have its effect. It always frightened her anew. Yet immediately afterward, she regained control. Like an angry snake hissing back, she replied, "This weakling. He has nothing but stone in his head. He must have mistaken her, otherwise I can't explain this!"
