Rebekka stared after him with her lips pressed into a sneer. Suddenly life seemed to return to her Rasta braids. They curled and slithered in snake-like movements as if a dozen small vipers were licking around the head of the teacher. A sinister hiss could be heard, which immediately died down again. Rebekka's crimson hair now shimmered dull, and she muttered a soft curse. Only then did she stiffen, squinting her eyes and gazing thoughtfully ahead.
