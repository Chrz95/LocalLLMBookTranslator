The old man nodded his head involuntarily. Oh yes, how many times had he experienced betrayal in his long life! How often had he learned that one of his followers could not resist the seductions of the enemy and secretly joined its service. This made him a particularly dangerous ally for the Dark, because an acknowledged foe is easier to fight than a pretended friend whose heart harbors treachery. That's why the forces of darkness constantly tried to win knights from Gralsburg to their side.
