Despite these efforts, the servants of darkness had still not managed to defeat the powers of light. Even as the conflicts became more frequent and intense - in this ongoing struggle between good and evil, fortune was always on the side of the light! At that thought, the old man's face lit up, and his watery blue eyes sparkled like those of a young man. Oh yes, he was still capable of fulfilling the task entrusted to him since the beginning of time! But perhaps it was slowly about time to think about a successor...
