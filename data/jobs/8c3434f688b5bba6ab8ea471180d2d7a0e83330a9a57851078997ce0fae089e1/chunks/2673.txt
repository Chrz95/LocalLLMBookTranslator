She took an iron pot from the cupboard, hurried to the sink, and quickly scooped water out of a kettle. When she had filled the pot halfway, she put on the lid and carried it over to the stove where she roughly placed it on the griddle above the fire hole, causing some water to spill over and sizzle loudly as it evaporated on the stove top. Alienor paid no mind to this. She added a piece of wood, dashed to the stock cabinet, and pulled out the drawer containing the herb mixture for Elysion’s sleeping potion. Quickly, she measured two spoons of herbs from the linen bag into an earthen cup.
