When the Guardian of Light saw the weapon, he was frightened to his core. He knew all too well about the cruel power of this sword, which had earned its name Pestilenz for no reason: forged by the dark elves beyond the Fire Mountains at the very beginning of time and imbued with demonic powers by the treacherous Fhurhurs, it was the only weapon that could bring him misfortune. And just when he was once again threatened by this terrible sword after countless ages, Elysion had no protection.
