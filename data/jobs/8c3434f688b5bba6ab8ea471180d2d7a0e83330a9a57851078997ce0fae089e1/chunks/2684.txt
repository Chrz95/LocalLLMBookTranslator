While the monotonous thumping of the hammer against Alienor's ear and the crackling of the wood in the hearth filled her ears, she lifted the lid of the pot with glowing cheeks as the water inside began to boil. The aromatic scent of the herbs in the cup wafted into her nose: sleeproot, feverfew, and dragon thistle. But the girl only took this in peripherally because her thoughts were elsewhere, with Alarik.
