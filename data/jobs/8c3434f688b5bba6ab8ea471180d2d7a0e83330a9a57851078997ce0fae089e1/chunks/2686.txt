Since Alarik left, the Swuupie had been acting strangely. It was probably his longing for his master that made him continuously emit heart-wrenching sounds. Even the most delicious treats could not soothe his sorrow. He kept scratching at Alienor's door and had tried to fly away through the window. So far, Alienor had barely managed to prevent it from happening. As her room was secluded, no one else had heard the spectacle of Smoochfeast. But it couldn't remain undiscovered for much longer.
