Lukas gave him the stone from the library. Percy weighed it briefly in his hand to estimate its weight. Then he raised the stone before his right eye, squinted slightly, and examined it closely. After that, he carefully ran his fingertips over the surface, held it under his nose, and sniffed at it. When Percy had finished examining it, he turned with a deliberate nod towards the children. "This is unmistakably Maltese marble," he said with certainty. "There's no doubt about it. This fragment must have come from the tomb chamber in the Old Vault! None of the other parts of the castle use such stone!"
