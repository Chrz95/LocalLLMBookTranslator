The moon emerged from behind the clouds and bathed the Hangman's Wood in pale light. Although Laura had only been there with her three friends a few days ago, it seemed even more menacing under the cover of night. The forest appeared to have taken on an eerie life of its own. Like an army of colossal prehistoric monsters, the ancient trees towered towards the sky and stretched out their countless branches in silent defiance. The bare bushes and shrubs between the mighty trunks resembled a ghostly ground force of dark orcs and vicious trolls, making it impossible to penetrate their ranks. Bizarre patterns and shadowy webs floated over the leaf-covered forest floor, and sounds were everywhere – rustling and whispering, creaking and growling, cracking and crackling in the undergrowth – yet there was no sign from where they came.
