The friends kept close together as they approached the crypt. Fortunately, Laura had thought to bring flashlights. Three strong beams illuminated the narrow footpath that snaked through the forest. Occasionally, one of the friends directed the light sideways, and the bright beam darted over trees and bushes. Moss spots on the trunks and long hanging mosses glowed ghostly pale, and every now and then a light beam was reflected by a gleaming pair of eyes – an animal on a nocturnal hunt. Perhaps it was a fox or a marten. But maybe other creatures were also on their way through the woods?
