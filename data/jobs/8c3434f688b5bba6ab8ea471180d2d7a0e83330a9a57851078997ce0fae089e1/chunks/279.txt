Borboron had already regained his equilibrium. His sword pierced the White Knight's parade. Then the Black Prince attacked in turn. But even with his first strike, he realized that he was facing a worthy opponent, despite being much younger and possibly less experienced than him. Borboron's pale face contorted into an angry grimace as the infernal fire in his eyes glowed more fiercely than ever while Paravain deftly parried Pestilence's blows before launching his own attack. An intense battle erupted between these two mismatched men. Neither was willing to retreat; both knew that it was a matter of life and death.
