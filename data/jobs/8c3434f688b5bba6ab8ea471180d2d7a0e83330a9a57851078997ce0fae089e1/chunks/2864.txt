The bailiff had already steered towards the entrance door when a sudden growl erupted from Groll. And then again. The gardener stood in surprise and turned around - only to see the crows. Far off, they circled in the night sky over the Hangman's Wood. Right above the Old Vault. Not one of them made a sound, but Albin Ellerking was suddenly acutely aware that there must be a reason for this unusual behavior.
