Suddenly, Laura spotted a small relief that had been carved into one of the stone blocks. It was located at knee height on the far right edge of the wall. She bent down in front of it and examined it closely. Lukas and Kaja stood behind her, leaning over her shoulder and also directing their lamps towards the relief. Now Laura could see that it was merely a fragment because precisely here a piece had been broken out from the stone. Where the upper half of the relief should have been, there was a small hole in the wall. Below this hole, she could make out the body of a horse along with its legs as well as the legs of two riders in armor. A semicircle of Latin words formed the outer edge of the fragmented engraving. Laura knew immediately what it was about.
