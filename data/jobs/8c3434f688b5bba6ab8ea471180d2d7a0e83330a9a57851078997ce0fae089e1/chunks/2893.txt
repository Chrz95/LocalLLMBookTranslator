She had an idea. She tried to fit the stone fragment back into its original place and pressed it into the hole in the wall, which went smoothly at first. But then the piece got stuck. It was still about half a centimeter out of the wall but wouldn't budge even with strong pressure. Laura quickly turned on her flashlight and hit the stone firmly with the handle of the lamp. And behold: The stone slid completely into the wall, and the seal was now complete. Nothing indicated anymore that there had been a part missing just moments before.
