Paravain knew too well what this wound inflicted by the sword of Pestilenz meant: Wounds that were struck with his blade never healed. The injured had to suffer a painful death if they were not treated promptly with the only effective remedy: the water of life. Only the elixir from the cup of enlightenment could still save their lord now. But the cup was in the possession of the enemies. No one knew where on the Celestial Sphere the Dark Powers hid it. Fear rose up in Paravain.
