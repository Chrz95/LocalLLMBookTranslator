With cautious steps, Laura, Lukas, and Kaja entered the chamber. It smelled musty, and the monotonous sound of water droplets splashing on the stone floor could be heard. The light from their flashlights danced through the darkness of the room. It was difficult to gauge its dimensions accurately, but Laura guessed that it was about fifteen meters long and ten meters wide. And the height of the chamber might match twice the length of a small man's body.
