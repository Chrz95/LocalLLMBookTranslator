As the three friends approached the sarcophagus, Laura let the beam of her lamp sweep over the side walls. It was true, as Percy had told them: The walls of the tomb chamber were entirely made of marble. The man-high stone coffin was also crafted from the same material. Relief carvings on its sides depicted scenes from chivalric life. Moreover, it was adorned with the coat of arms and insignia of the Ravenstein family. An inscription at the base confirmed that this indeed was Reimar von Ravenstein's last resting place.
