Kaja swiveled her flashlight - and immediately in front of her face, she saw a revolting creature: A fat, disgusting spider was dangling right before her nose from the ceiling! Full of panic, Kaja screamed out like someone had been stung by a sting, and recoiled in horror until she hit something man-high. It sounded metallic, and at that very moment, two heavy, cold arms wrapped around her shoulders. A massive monster tried to push her down to the ground! When Kaja saw the pale skeletal hands at the ends of the iron arms, she felt terror. The bony hands were twisted into terrifying claws.
