Kaja sighed in relief when she realized that the iron monster was merely an old armor. The helmet, with its decayed remnants of the once proud crest hanging askew and positioned next to her head, slowly turned towards her as she cautiously peered through the open visor into the interior of the helmet. A pale skull, with a few shriveled skin flaps still clinging to it, glared at her with toothless grin. From the dark recesses of its left eye, another spider crawled out and scurried down Kaja's shoulder, hurriedly making its way towards the ground. Shaken by cold dread, she let out another shrill scream.
