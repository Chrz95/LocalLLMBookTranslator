»Wait a moment, I want to try something«, said Lukas. He walked towards the torch and took out a lighter from his pocket, struck it, and held the flame to the torch. And indeed: After only a short time, the piece of cloth soaked with pitch caught fire and began to blaze. The flickering light cast the tomb chamber into half-light, and now the friends could see that there were no other burial sites in the crypt besides Reimar von Ravenstein's sarcophagus. Just as lonely as he had lived his life, the Cruel Knight also lay buried alone—except for the four unfortunate souls who kept him company in eternal sleep.
