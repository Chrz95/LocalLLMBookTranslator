But Laura did not let herself be deterred. She was sure that she had not been mistaken. Quickly, she took a few steps towards the wall and placed her ear against it to listen. Suddenly, she noticed that her brother Lukas again approached the altar with the Grail image, completely carefree sticking his head into it to get a closer look at the vessel containing the deadly contents. She straightened up and called out: "Be careful, Lukas! Remember what Percy told us about the gruesome end of the knight. Don't let that happen to you!"
