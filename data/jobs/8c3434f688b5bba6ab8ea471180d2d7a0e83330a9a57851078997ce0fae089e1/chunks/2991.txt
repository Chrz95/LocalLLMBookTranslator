Laura didn't know about it. She watched the gardener anxiously as he entered the tomb with angular movements, took a torch from its holder, and menacingly advanced towards her friends. They retreated. Ellerking pushed them further into the corner until they finally stood with their backs against the wall. Laura tried to escape by going around the gardener on his right side. But Albin was on guard and struck at her with the burning torch. The girl recoiled and barely managed to dodge the flaming wick. Lukas also wanted to flee from Ellerking, but he had no more success than his sister. In fact: the torch burned a tuft of hair off him and even damaged part of the shell of his right ear.
