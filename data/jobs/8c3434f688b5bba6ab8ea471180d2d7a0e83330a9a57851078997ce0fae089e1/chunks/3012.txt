The large crows perched on the trees suddenly became wide-awake. Instantly, the winged guardians of the grave site let out a deafening screech. They flapped their wings wildly and followed the fleeing ones who ran for their lives through the nightmarish executioner's forest. Laura, Kaja, and Lukas could hear the fluttering of the wings, and the eerie "Krah-Krah" of the birds rang in their ears. But they did not turn around; instead, they kept running further away. Fearfully, they drew their heads in and expected sharp beak attacks at any moment.
