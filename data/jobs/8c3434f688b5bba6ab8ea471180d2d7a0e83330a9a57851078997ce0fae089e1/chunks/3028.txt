Albin Ellerking snorted angrily and grimaced in pain as his face contorted. His left leg burned like fire. When he stumbled over the knight's armor and fell to the ground, not only had he torn his pants on the rusty metal, but he had also ripped his shinbone all along its length with blood. And this damned fog had taken away any sight for him, so that he lost orientation and ran into the wall several times. This resulted in painful bruises. Fortunately, the blinding haze suddenly disappeared.
