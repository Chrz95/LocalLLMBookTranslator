Suddenly life entered the boxwood trees. They rustled loudly and began to deform themselves. The leaves fused more and more together until both dog shapes were covered by a closed dark green canopy. A dull rumbling and a hoarse barking could be heard as the transformation of the boxwoods progressed. From the leafy canopy, shiny black dog fur formed, beneath which strong muscles became visible. Veins became apparent, and eyes that had been lifeless suddenly sparkled viciously in the moonlight. The motionless tails started to wag at any moment, furry paws dug into the earth and tore themselves from the grass with a squelch: The two harmless boxwood dogs had transformed into two dangerous dogs made of flesh and blood.
