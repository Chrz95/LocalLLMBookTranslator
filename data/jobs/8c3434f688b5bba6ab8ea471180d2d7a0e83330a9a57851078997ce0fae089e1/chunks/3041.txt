The enormous dogs raised their heads, turning their pointed ears to the wind and taking in scents. They opened their mouths and bared murderous fangs. Saliva dripped from their lips as a deep growl echoed out. Once again they stretched and widened themselves, as if they wanted to rid themselves of the stiffness of their plant-like existence forever. Another shrill whistle came from the Executioner's Forest, and Dragan and Drogur, the ravenous dogs of the Dark Powers, dashed away with loud barks.
