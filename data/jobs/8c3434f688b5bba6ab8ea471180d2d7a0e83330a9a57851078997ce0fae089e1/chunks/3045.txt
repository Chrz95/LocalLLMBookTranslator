Alarik crawled close to the ground, trying to avoid the poisonous fumes of the sulfur pool. Despite this, he felt a burning in his throat, his eyes stung, and his knees and hands ached as sweat poured from every pore. The unfamiliar posture was extremely tiring and made him move forward slowly. Yet, Alarik resisted the temptation to sit up. Silvan had instructed him to crawl only. The advice of the woodsman had proven correct so far and guided him safely through the swamp. Only a few times did he stray an arm's length or slightly more off the path. Immediately his arms and legs sank into the mire, but each time Alarik managed to find solid ground again.
