Alarik paused, tilted his head back, peered up, and listened intently. But he saw nothing; the poisonous yellow mist still obscured his vision. Just as he was about to continue crawling forward, he heard that strange rustling again. It grew louder, and at the same time, a sound echoed that sent shivers down Alarik's spine: the mocking laughter of a woman. The young man jumped back in fright, but it had already faded away. Only the constant bubbling of the swamp persisted.
