The fear paralysed Alarik's mind, and so he stumbled on blindly until his strength gave out and he collapsed. He no longer felt the dampness and wetness in his clothes. His heart beat so wildly that the thudding beats in his head hurt. The lungs burned as if a fire was raging within them. The young man gasped for breath, struggling to catch his air. He had been so preoccupied with himself that he almost missed the voice.
