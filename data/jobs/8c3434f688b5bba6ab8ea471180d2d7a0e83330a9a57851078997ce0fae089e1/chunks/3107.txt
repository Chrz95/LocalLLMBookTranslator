The Dalmatians shuddered and were instantly transformed as if by magic. Overwhelmed with renewed aggression, they barked at the girl. Their tails whipped through the air. With their front paws, claws long and sharp, pressed into the grass, they crouched low to the ground like panthers before an attack. Then they shot forward swiftly like arrows toward their friends - but abruptly halted in mid-attack. Their menacing growls had turned into a frightened whimper. Startled, the black dogs stared in the direction of the children only to turn around seconds later. With tails tucked between their legs, they fled in long strides.
