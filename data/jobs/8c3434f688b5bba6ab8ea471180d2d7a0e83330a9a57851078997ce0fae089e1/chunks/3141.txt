Astonished, the lad watched the birds fly away until they disappeared from his gaze. He had already felt lost, but when Pfeilschwinge suddenly appeared, it had given him new hope for life. And now, the eagle, the messenger of light, had simply abandoned him! Pfeilschwinge must have known that he was lost without any help and must have noticed that Paravain’s lad was up to his chin in the swamp and couldn’t move anymore! And yet, he had just left him behind!
