Already a boundless emptiness was beginning to spread within Alarik when he realized that it was futile to struggle against his fate. He had to reconcile himself with his inevitable destiny. The boy closed his eyes. In the last moments of his life, his thoughts should dwell on those closest to his heart: on Alienor, his sister; and father and mother and the other siblings; on Paravain and Morwena, and naturally also on Elysion, the Guardian of Light. He would never see them again, and he would miss them very much in the Eternal Darkness.
