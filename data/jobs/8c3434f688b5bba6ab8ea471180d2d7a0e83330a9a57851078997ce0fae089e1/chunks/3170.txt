She once again directed her gaze at the oil painting. No, she had not been mistaken - the black wolf was completely gone from it. And if Lukas and Kaja could not recognize this, then it must be because they were not guardians. They could only perceive the outer appearance of things but not discern what lay hidden beneath the surface. And suddenly Laura had a terrifying thought: Perhaps Lukas and Kaja are also unable to see the Cup of Knowledge? Maybe it remains hidden from their eyes just as much as the changes on the painting do? But how can they then help me in my search?
