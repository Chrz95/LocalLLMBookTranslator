When Laura stepped into the hallway, her eyes widened: in front of her bedroom door, a woman was pacing back and forth. She was dressed in a floor-length white gown and sobbed quietly to herself. Laura couldn't immediately recognize her because she had her back turned, but when the figure turned around, she saw that it was Silva, the young woman from the painting in the hall. Her pale face showed as usual deep sorrow, and now tears rolled down her cheeks. Her footsteps were not audible as she approached Laura.
