Aravain stood at the window of the throne room and gazed out into the darkness. But the young knight did not notice what was happening on the night-highlands of Calderan. His gaze was directed into nothingness, and his face was marked by deep helplessness. Never had he felt so helpless in all his life as during these hours. Completely helpless and simultaneously condemned to inaction. Elysion, the Guardian of Light, lay dying. Aventerra and the Man-Star were threatened with destruction, and he, Paravain, could do nothing. Not a thing!
