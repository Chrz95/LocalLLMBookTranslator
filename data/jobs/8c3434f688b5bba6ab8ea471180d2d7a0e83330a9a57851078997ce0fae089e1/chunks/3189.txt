Confused thoughts ran through the knight's mind. How many times had he already tortured his brain, back and forth over whether there might still be a way to intervene. But in the end, he always came to the same conclusion: He could do nothing to influence the course of fate. The cup of enlightenment was hidden on the Manstern, and the path to it was blocked from him. The magical portal was closed. Until it opened, there was only one thing he could do.
