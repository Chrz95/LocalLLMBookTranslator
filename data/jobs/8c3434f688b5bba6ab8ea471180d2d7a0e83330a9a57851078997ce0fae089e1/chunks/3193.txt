This condition made Paravain almost ill. And what was even worse: He increasingly doubted that the rule of the Eternal Nothing could be prevented at all. After all, their fate lay solely in the hands of a girl. Of course - Laura had been born under the sign of Thirteen and possessed very special powers. But she had only recently joined the ranks of the Watchers, so her abilities hadn't fully developed yet. The girl only had a small part of the magical powers at her disposal, and it was even questionable whether she already knew how to apply them correctly. Perhaps it would fail just as Alarik did?
