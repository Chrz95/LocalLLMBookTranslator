The thought of his squire nearly brought tears to Paravain's eyes. Although Pfeilschwinge had already conveyed the happy news that the boy was safe and traveling with Silvan back towards Hellunyat, the fact that Alarik had been driven to such a mad venture by his despair nearly broke his heart. It was inconceivable what might have happened if Alarik had become Syrin's victim! Paravain was certain that no one else but Borboron's closest confidants in the guise of the harpy had hidden themselves there.
