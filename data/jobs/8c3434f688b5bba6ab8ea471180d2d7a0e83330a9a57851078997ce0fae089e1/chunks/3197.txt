The uncertainty drove Paravain almost to madness, and so he decided on a step that he had previously shied away from: At the onset of nightfall, he would send his shadow down upon the Human Star. The formless creature could find its way through the magical portal even outside it. There, the shadow was condemned to inactivity; it was nothing more than an observer who could neither intervene nor effect anything. But at least after returning, the shadow could report back and provide him with certainty about whether there still was hope for rescue.
