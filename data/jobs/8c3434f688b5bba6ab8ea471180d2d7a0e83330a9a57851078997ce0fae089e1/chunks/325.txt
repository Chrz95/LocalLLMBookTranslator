"Believe me, Laura, you've only dreamed all of this," Lukas said, while he chewed his bread with full cheeks. A red spot from the jam was on the corner of his right mouth, and a small dab of cottage cheese stuck to his chin. Cottage cheese with strawberry jam was his favorite topping. "It was nothing but a dream," he repeated in a dull voice. "Exactly like yesterday's story about knights and the chalice!"
