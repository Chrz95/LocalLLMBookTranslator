Laura cast a glance over her shoulder and made sure the rest of the class didn't overhear her conversation with Percy. However, the classmates lag quite far behind, and Kaja and Max Stinkefurz were even the distant last runners in the gasping field of runners. No one could beat Laura's weekly cross-country run except for Laura herself. With a visibly reluctant pace, her classmates strolled through the park at their own leisurely speed and made no attempt to catch up with Laura and Percy. After all, they had not been running particularly fast either. They hadn't even caught their breath yet, which was no wonder given how slowly everyone else was moving.
