They turned around the corner of the main building and headed towards the entrance. On the large lawn in the background stood the intern's gardener. He was standing next to the two buxus hounds. Laura couldn't quite see what he was doing there, but it seemed almost as if he were talking to them. Why not? After all, she had heard many times that gardeners talked to their plants. Supposedly, this was good for their growth. So why shouldn't Albin Ellerking also talk to his buxus dogs? However, she found it a bit odd that he stroked their backs almost tenderly while doing so.
