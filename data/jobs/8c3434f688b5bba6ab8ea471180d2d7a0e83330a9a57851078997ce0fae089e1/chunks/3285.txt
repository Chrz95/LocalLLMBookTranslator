Side by side, Laura and Percy walked across the narrow wooden bridge that spanned the moat of the castle, then turned into a path that led to the monument. As they passed the dense shrubbery that stood just behind the fork on the edge of the road, neither of them noticed the dark figure hiding behind it. It was Attila Morduk, the housekeeper. With a grim face, the bald man watched them until they had disappeared from his sight.
