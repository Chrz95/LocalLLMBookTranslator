Nothing happened at all. But then a dull rumbling and creaking could be heard. The column began to tremble barely perceptibly at first, but increasingly violently. Laura stepped back in fright and stared at the stone giant, which seemed to come alive gradually. He jerked and shuddered, and his limbs moved. At first slowly, then more strongly. The giant groaned as he loosened his grip on the front awning with his hands and finally let go completely. The entire figure of the giant appeared to shrink.
