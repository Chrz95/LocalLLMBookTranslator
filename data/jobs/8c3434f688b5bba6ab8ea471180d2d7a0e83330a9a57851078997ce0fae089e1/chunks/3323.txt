But for that, the man from the Maurenland had come up with something else to keep unwanted visitors away from the treasure chamber. He had cleverly pretended the collapse of the tunnel, as his friends soon found out. They barely turned a corner when they were already standing at what seemed like the unlucky spot. It looked so real: The ceiling had collapsed over five meters in length, the walls had caved in, and stones, shattered support beams, and wall remnants piled up to man-high, making any further advance almost impossible. No wonder Professor Morgenstern and Percy Valiant fell for this deception and didn’t even try to continue their search for the chalice inside the tunnel.
