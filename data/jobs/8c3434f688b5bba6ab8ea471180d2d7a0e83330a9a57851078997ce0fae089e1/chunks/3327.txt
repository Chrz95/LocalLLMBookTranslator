The friends had to take cover so as not to be hit by the wild flying chunks of debris. Laura watched in awe from her hiding spot as the Stone Giant cleared away the rubble heap with a ridiculous speed. After making an initial breakthrough, he used a thick support beam as his tool. In this way, he accomplished the task much faster than before. It only took a few minutes for Portak to have already driven a hole of more than one meter in diameter into the pile of rocks. He paused briefly and examined his work. Then he looked around at Laura. Surprise entered his gaze when he could not find her anywhere.
