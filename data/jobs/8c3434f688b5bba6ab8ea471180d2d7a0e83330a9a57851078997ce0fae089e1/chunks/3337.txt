With the blueprint in one hand and the flashlight in the other, Laura walked forward through the tunnel-like corridor. The light beam cut like a sharp finger of illumination through the darkness and flickered over the stony walls and tiled floors covered with dust and sand. The air down in the Burgkeller was less humid than that in the Old Crypt, but it was still stuffy and smelled similarly. Here too, an odor of death and decay rose up, and Laura felt a slight queasiness.
