Suddenly the ground beneath her feet gave way. Laura stepped into thin air and fell into the abyss. With a sudden scream she threw her arms up in desperation and searched frantically for something to hold onto. Her flashlight slipped from her right hand, crashing noisily into the dark yawning chasm below, and her hands reached for nothingness. However, at the last moment Laura managed to grab a narrow ledge and clung on with all her might. The whole thing had taken only fractions of a second, but it felt like an eternity to Laura.
