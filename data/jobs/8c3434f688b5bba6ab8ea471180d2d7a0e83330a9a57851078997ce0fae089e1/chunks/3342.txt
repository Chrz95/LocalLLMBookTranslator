When she lowered her gaze, she could see in the light of the flashlight lying on the ground that she was dangling over a pit about ten meters deep. A secret trapdoor in the floor of the tunnel had hidden Laura's eyes and opened under her weight. The bottom of the pit was littered with long iron rods whose sharp tips were thrusting towards her. Three irregularly shaped bundles of rags hung on these spikes. They were torn and decayed. Suddenly, Laura realized what it was all about, and a scream of horror escaped her lips.
