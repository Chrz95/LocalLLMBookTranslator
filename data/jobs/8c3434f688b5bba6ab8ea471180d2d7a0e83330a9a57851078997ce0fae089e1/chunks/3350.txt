The evening turned out much better than Percy and Mary had dared to hope. Dr. Quintus Schwartz had proven himself an exceptionally charming host, drawing his guests into a lively conversation and infecting them with his sparkling mood. Even Rebekka Taxus had shown a likable side. The colleague had emerged as an extremely intelligent and multifaceted conversationalist, which Percy and Mary found quite astonishing given that she usually presented herself with sharp tongue and bad temper in the faculty and before students.
