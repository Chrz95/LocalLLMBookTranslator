She had been most enthusiastic about the food, though. Percy could not remember the last time he had eaten so well; therefore, he did not want to believe it at first when Quintus Schwartz revealed that he himself had prepared this exquisite meal personally. After all, such a menu would have honored any four-star chef. However, Quintus had strictly refused to give away the recipe. He had only smiled and said it was a specialty of his establishment, but he had not divulged more than that. Percy did not press him further and instead turned his attention to the wine—because it was even more delicious than the dishes themselves.
