But the unease that had befallen her was already driven away with the next breath. Hardly had they set aside the oil painting when Laura planted herself squarely in front of the empty wall and examined attentively the hewn stone blocks from which it was constructed. The outlines of the painting were clearly visible. Where it had hung, the stones were lighter than the rest. At first glance, there was nothing unusual about this surface, but then Laura did notice something.
