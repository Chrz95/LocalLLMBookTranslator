Quickly, Laura moved one of the chairs under a stone, climbed onto it, and pressed firmly with her thumb on the seal. In the next moment, it became clear that she had correctly guessed: As in the crypt, there was suddenly a rumbling and shaking. It came closer and grew louder. It sounded almost like a heavy bowling ball slowly rolling towards them. When the noise was very close, a piece of wall swung back as if moved by an invisible hand, creating a narrow passage in the wall that revealed a tube-like corridor facing their dark friends.
