Before the entrance portal, he straightened up. He paused and stared at the mighty door, and it almost seemed as if he was surprised that it was closed. Then he looked around – at least the outline of his head against the portal gave this impression. After a while, it appeared that he had discovered what he was looking for. He stepped next to the portal – and began climbing up the wall. With complete ease, he moved upward, and although ivy covered the wall densely, not the slightest sound could be heard.
