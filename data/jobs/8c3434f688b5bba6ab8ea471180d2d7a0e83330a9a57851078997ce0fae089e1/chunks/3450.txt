In the entrance hall, the shadow crawled along the walls. When he saw the painting leaning against the wall, he stopped. Then he spotted the opening of the secret passage leading to the treasure chamber. Quickly, he made his way towards it. It already seemed as if he wanted to disappear into it, but then he changed his mind. He turned around and retreated under the staircase that led up to the upper floors of the girls' wing. He almost merged completely with the darkness, and only the sharp eyes of a nocturnal creature would have been able to discern there now that something mysterious was lurking. It stood there quite calmly and waited.
