Not a single second too late, because already the first blow of the knight struck against the blunt blade that had rusted over centuries. Sparks flew up as edge met edge. A sudden pain shot through Laura's arm. The force of the strike was so great that she thought for a moment her wrist was broken. The skullcracker flared again, and once more Laura managed to deflect the attack. She wanted to counterattack, but there was no way. The sword was much heavier than the usual rapier. She had to hold it with both hands and strain all her strength to wield it and fend off the knight's attacks.
