Almost incredulously, Laura stared at the rubble field. When she finally averted her gaze, she noticed that she was trembling all over. Her knees felt as soft as wax. Sweat streamed down her face and stung in her eyes. The pain in her wrist bones stabbed like a scalding hot thorn into her flesh. Suddenly, she remembered the shadow again. But no matter how much she looked around – he had vanished without a trace.
