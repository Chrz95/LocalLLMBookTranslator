On the ground floor, Laura stood still and cast a hesitant glance at the remnants of Reimar von Ravenstein, scattered at the foot of the stairs. Without thinking, she pressed the chalice closer to herself. It almost seemed as if she was afraid that even now the knight could snatch it from her hands.

The stone figure had shattered into so many fragments that it was barely possible to assign the pieces to a specific body part. Only the right hand of the knight could be clearly seen by Laura. The ring and middle finger were broken off and lay almost two meters away from the hand on the ground.
