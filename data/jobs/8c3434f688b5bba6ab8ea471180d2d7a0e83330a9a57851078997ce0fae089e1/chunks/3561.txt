Laura had barely disappeared up the stairs when the broken index finger of the knight began to twitch. It curled and shot forward a little bit as if there was still some life left in it. Even the ring finger moved. At first it trembled barely, then the tip bent slowly until the dirty fingernail scraped across the stone floor with a creaking sound. As though on a secret command, the severed fingers began to move. Driven by invisible forces, they crawled like fat maggots purposefully towards the rest of the right hand - and almost it seemed as if they wanted to reunite with her again.
