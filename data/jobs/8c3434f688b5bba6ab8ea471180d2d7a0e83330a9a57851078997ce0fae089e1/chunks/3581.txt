"Can't you figure this out yourself, you money-saving Kiwi?" Lukas knelt into the empty half of the wardrobe and felt along the bottom plate with his fingers. After a few seconds, he found what he was looking for. He pressed on a button hidden in the plate, Laura heard a loud "Click," and then the back wall of the wardrobe detached from its hinges. Lukas lifted it out of the holder and set it aside, and there Laura saw that the wardrobe had a double back panel with a spacious secret compartment hidden inside.
