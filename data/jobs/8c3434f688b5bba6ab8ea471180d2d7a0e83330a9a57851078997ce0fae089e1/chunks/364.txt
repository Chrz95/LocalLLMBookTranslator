Sayelle gasped for air like a stranded fish. Then she raised her eyebrows and furrowed her brow. For a moment it looked as though she was about to let loose a terrible tirade, but even though Lukas had nothing to fear, he instinctively drew his head in. Laura, on the other hand, looked at her stepmother calmly. She didn’t care if she screamed and raged as much as she wanted—she didn’t give a damn!
