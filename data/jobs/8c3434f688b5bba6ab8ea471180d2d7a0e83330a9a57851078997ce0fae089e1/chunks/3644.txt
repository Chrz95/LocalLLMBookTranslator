The plain was almost entirely covered by dense black fog. Not the slightest trace of the landscapes in the distance could be seen - neither from the Modermoor to the east nor from the Drachenberges to the south or from the Raunewald to the west. In their place, only darkness reigned. It would not take long for the Black Fog and the Eternal Darkness also to reach the walls of Hellunyat and engulf the Grail Castle.
