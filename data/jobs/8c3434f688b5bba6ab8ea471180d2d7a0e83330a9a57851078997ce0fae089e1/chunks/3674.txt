When the gate of the dungeon behind the girls closed, Laura also lost her last bit of hope. Although she had no idea where exactly their windowless prison was located, an inner voice immediately told her that they would certainly not be found in this dark cell. Dr. Schwartz had blindfolded them before leading them into the dungeon. Even though Laura could see nothing at all, she tried to memorize the path they walked back and forth. But already after a short time, she completely lost orientation. The only thing she was sure of was that they must be in the crypts of the castle. They had descended step by step and then marched along seemingly endless corridors until Dr. Schwartz finally took off their blindfolds and threw them into the dungeon.
