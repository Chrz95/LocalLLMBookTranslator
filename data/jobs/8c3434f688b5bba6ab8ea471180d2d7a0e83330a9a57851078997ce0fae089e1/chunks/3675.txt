The room, which was only illuminated by two torches, was damp and cold and measured perhaps five by six meters. Two simple wooden bunks with coarse blankets lay on them were the only furnishings. The walls were constructed of rough stone and so thick that even the loudest cries for help could not penetrate them. The grille that separated her cell from the corridor was made of solid iron bars. They were as thick as an arm, and it would probably take even Portak with his immense strength to bend them. Even the door that Dr. Quintus Schwarz had just locked made escape seem hopeless.
