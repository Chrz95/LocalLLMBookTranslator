The sun rose in the sky, and on the oldest of the ancient planets, the day of the Winter Solstice dawned. Knight Paravain stood atop the great tower of Grail Castle and cast his gaze over the surroundings. But no matter which direction he looked to the heavens - nothing could be seen from the original landscapes of Aventerra anymore. The land was completely enveloped in a dense black fog, and only the walls and turrets of Hellunyat protruded above the sea of mist.
