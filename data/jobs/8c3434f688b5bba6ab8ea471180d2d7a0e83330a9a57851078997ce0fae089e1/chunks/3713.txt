The young knight sighed. This is the end, he thought. Of course, he knew that a whole day and an entire night lay ahead of them, and it was still possible to avert the impending fate within the remaining time. But since his shadow had returned to him and reported on its excursion to the Human Star, he knew there was almost no hope for rescue anymore. What good would it do to weigh oneself in hope when not even the slightest reason existed for it?
