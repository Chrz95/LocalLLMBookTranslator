The terrifying aspect of the current situation was that they were not dealing with a tangible opponent whom one could confront and stop. The Nothing was invincible because it drew its strength solely from the increasingly diminishing powers of Elysion. In the same measure as the Guardian of Light lost life energy, the Nothing grew stronger. Paravain and his White Knights were helpless; they were condemned to inaction and could only wait until they were swallowed by the Eternal Nothing.
