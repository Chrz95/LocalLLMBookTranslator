The young knight understood the message immediately that the eagle had brought to him: It was time for him to set out with the other White Knights on their journey into the Valley of Time beyond the Thundering Mountains. For there, in the homeland of Pfeilschwinge, the magical portal between Aventerra and the Star of Man would take shape at the first ray of sunlight, as it had done every one of the four Sun Festivals since time immemorial. At nightfall, this portal would open again, allowing them to bring back the Chalice of Enlightenment from Aventerra.

Although the Black Mist now covered all of Aventerra, the Grail Castle and the Valley of Time remained untouched by it because the power of light still held sway there. But that too would come to an end when the sun rose tomorrow morning. And although Paravain no longer had any hope, he would heed the call of the portal and wait with his White Knights for its return—forever. For he was certain that no one would come to bring him the Chalice of Enlightenment.
