He Swuupie hopped over the hilltop, skipped across the sunlit meadow, stood on its hind legs and peered curiously around with its button eyes. The furry head bobbed excitedly back and forth as it sniffed the air. Standing upright, the creature might have measured a little less than an ell in height. It had a surprising resemblance to a weasel: Its eyes were surrounded by black spots, and its gray-brown fur shone silky in the light. The black-and-gray ringed, bushy tail was about half as long as the body and constantly in motion. However, its ears reminded one of those of a teddy bear, and on its back it carried wings, thin membranes that could be spread out like bat wings or folded up.
