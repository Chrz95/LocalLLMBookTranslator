A bright boy's voice broke through the silence: "Schmatzfraß! Schmaatzfraaß!! Where are you?" Immediately after, a tall boy emerged from behind the hill, leading a brown prairie pony by its reins. He might have been fourteen summers old. He was barefoot and wore a jerkin and knee-length breeches, both made of brown leather. Accompanying him was a girl in an simple white dress that reached down to her knees. She was younger, had the same regular facial features as the boy, and also blonde hair. However, hers were much longer and braided into thick pigtails.
