Laura looked around frantically but saw nothing with which she could repel or drive away the rats. Suddenly, an idea came to her. With a quick jerk, she pulled the blanket off the wooden bunk and rolled it up. Like a giant slingshot, she let the cumbersome fabric roll spin and struck at the disgusting creatures. The rats deftly dodged the blows and quickly retreated, only to return again in no time. The nimble rodents seemed to know exactly that the blanket could not harm them. Worse still – it appeared as if Laura's attacks were increasingly angering them. The leader of the pack let out a shrill squeal that sounded like a menacing threat. Then she stood on her hind legs and emitted a hoarse hiss. In addition, she bared her teeth.
