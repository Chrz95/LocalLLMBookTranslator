She crept to the wooden table by the lattice door, grabbed a few slices of sausage from the plate and threw them in the direction of the rats. They immediately pounced on them, using their strong teeth greedily to bite into the treats and tear large pieces off. With forceful bites they defended their loot against fellow rats who wanted to take it away from them. Laura watched with disgust as the rats scurried around in a frenzy, consuming the sausage almost entirely within a short time.
