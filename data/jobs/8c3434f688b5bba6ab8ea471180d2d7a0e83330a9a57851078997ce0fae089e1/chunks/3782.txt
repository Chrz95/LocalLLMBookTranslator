Laura closed her eyes and gathered herself. Then she fixed her gaze firmly on the key and commanded it with the strength of her mind to move. She imagined how he would pull loose from the hook, fall to the ground, and slide across the stone tiles towards her - alone, but it wouldn't work. Nothing happened. The large key hung motionless on the wall without making a single sound. The squeaking of the rats, which were fighting over food in the background, sounded like mocking laughter now.
