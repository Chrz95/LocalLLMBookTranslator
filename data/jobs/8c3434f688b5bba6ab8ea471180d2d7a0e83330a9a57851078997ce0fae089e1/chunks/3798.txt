Lukas was really worried. That he hadn't seen Laura and Kaja all day was still something to explain. After all, they were supposed to be studying for the physics test. But when neither of them had shown up for lunch as well, he had indeed been a bit surprised. Such things weren't his usual experience. Not with Kaja in particular. If Lukas remembered correctly, she hadn't missed any meal during her entire time at Ravenstein. After all, there was almost nothing more important to Kaja than eating. It must really be serious for Laura and Kaja if they even skipped lunch because of studying. Given their poor grades, that was also a good thing in Lukas's opinion, although he had wished he could have searched for the gate as quickly as possible. But he had resisted the temptation to urge the girls on and had spent the afternoon at his computer instead.
