When he received no reply again, he decided to push the door handle down abruptly. The door opened without any trouble. Lukas stepped in and stood there rooted to the spot. The room of the girls looked as if a tornado had just passed through it. The doors of the wardrobes and desks were wide open, and their contents scattered on the floor. The drawers were pulled out and clearly rummaged through, and even the mattresses had been torn from the beds. Whoever had searched this room must have done quite a bit of work.
