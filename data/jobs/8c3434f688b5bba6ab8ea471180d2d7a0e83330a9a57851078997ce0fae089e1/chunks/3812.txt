There, Lukas Kajas discovered his phone lying amidst the chaos on the floor. He bent down and picked it up. As he slipped it into his pocket, he thought he detected a familiar scent in his nose. He flared his nostrils, took a breath, and sniffed. It was only a faint whiff of musk that he could discern. But the boy was certain that only the Gruftie perfume from Taxus had left this scent trail behind. The teacher must have been in the room not too long ago, and for Lukas it was clear: Only Taxus could have thoroughly searched everything, and Dr. Schwartz might have helped her with that. Fortunately, neither of them had found what they were looking for, because for Lukas there was no doubt about what they sought after.
