Lukas came to the conclusion quite quickly that there were essentially only two reasonable answers to this question: either Laura and Kaja had been surprised by the Dark Ones and taken into their grasp - or they had recognized the danger in time and fled to Percy Valiant or Miss Mary. Otherwise, there was no one on the castle who they could have confided in. Of course, him himself included – but it was probably not their intention to put him and the chalice at risk either. He therefore needed to go to Percy as soon as possible.
