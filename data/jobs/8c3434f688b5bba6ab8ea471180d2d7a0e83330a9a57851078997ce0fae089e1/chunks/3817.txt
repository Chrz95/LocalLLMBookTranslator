Lukas stared at Attila Morduk uncertainly. He didn't know what to do because he simply wasn't sure if he could trust the caretaker. Although no student had ever said anything bad about Attila, neither had anyone said anything good. Most people feared him. This was only understandable in light of his dark expression as he always prowled around with a grimace on his face. Should he really entrust himself to such an important matter with someone who seemed so distrustful?
