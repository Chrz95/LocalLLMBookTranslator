The girl turned her head and tried to get an overview of the dimly lit vault where she helplessly dangled from the ceiling. This was not so easy, because Laura's hair hung in front of her eyes. But despite the hairy curtain, she could see that near her a huge iron chandelier with flickering candles was hanging. It too was held by a rope that ran over a pulley on the ceiling. The other end of the rope was fastened to a hook on the wall. The chandelier, which cast an eerie light in the room, could be adjusted in height, although Laura didn't know what good it would do.
