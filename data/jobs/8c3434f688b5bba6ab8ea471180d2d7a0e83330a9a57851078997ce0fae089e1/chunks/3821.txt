The torture chamber had to be on the same level as the dungeon where Dr. Schwartz and the Taxus had held them captive at night. On their way here, they hadn't passed a staircase, and the corridors neither went up nor down. They were therefore still deep underground, which meant that no one would hear their cries. Neither their pleas for help nor the pain-filled screams that would come out of their mouths as soon as the Cruel Knight began torturing them. For Reimar von Ravenstein seemed to have had this in mind exactly.
