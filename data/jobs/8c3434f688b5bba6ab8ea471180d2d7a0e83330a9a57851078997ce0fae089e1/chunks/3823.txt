"No, not! Please don't!" Kaja pleaded with the Cruel Knight as he closed her foot shackles with practiced movements. It was quite evident that she wasn't his first victim of torture. The girl struggled as well as she could and writhed on the hard wooden rack. But it was useless; the Cruel Knight held Kaja firmly in an unyielding grip and forced her wrists into iron fetters, which were connected to a stretching wheel by chains.
