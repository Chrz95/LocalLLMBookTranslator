It already seemed as though Laura's efforts would be in vain when the knot finally loosened. It jerked several times before it gave way, and in the next instant, the chandelier shot down with incredible force. With a loud hiss, the rope flew over the pulley. The Cruel Knight looked startled up. When he saw the chandelier rushing towards him, he tried to dodge to the side, but it was too late. At the blink of an eye, the hundred-weight load hit him on the head. Struck like a lightning bolt, the stone Reimar fell to the ground, and the chandelier crushed him with a crash above him.
