She broke off because suddenly a suppressed groan was heard behind her. The girls turned around and stared in shock at the chandelier that almost completely covered the Cruel Knight. Only his feet and right hand were still protruding underneath it. The iron ring with the wrought-iron candle holders had bent and buckled. Most of the candles had fallen from their holders and broken into pieces during the fall. Scattered on the ground or on the stone form of the knight, some of them still burned. Small rivulets of melted wax flowed over Reimar's granite body, which was barely damaged. As far as the girls could tell, only the ring finger and the index finger from his right hand had been severed and lay a few centimeters away from her.
