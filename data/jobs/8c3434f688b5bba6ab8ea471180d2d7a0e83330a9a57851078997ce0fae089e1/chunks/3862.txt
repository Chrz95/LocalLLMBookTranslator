Laura was also motionless in fear. A cold shiver ran down her spine, and on the top of her head she felt a tingling like an ant hill while she watched intently as the ring finger and the index finger of the knight rejoined with his hand. Reimar clenched his right fist as if to test it. His fingers obeyed him as though they had never been separated. The knight let out a contented groan and straightened up.
