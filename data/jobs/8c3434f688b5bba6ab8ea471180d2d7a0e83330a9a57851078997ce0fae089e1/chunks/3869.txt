The friends entered a long, vaulted corridor. It was narrow and dimly lit by torches that were mounted at larger intervals on wrought-iron wall brackets. As Laura and Kaja dashed ahead hand-in-hand, she noticed in the flickering twilight that an abundance of old weapons lined the walls: gleaming swords, iron lances, and sparkling halberds. Even morning stars with murderous iron thorns were present. It looked as though the area they were currently in had once served as a munition storehouse for the castle.
