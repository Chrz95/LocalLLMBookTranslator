Finally, they had reached the end of the corridor that now made a nearly right-angle turn to the right. As they rounded the corner, what greeted them was an oddly familiar sight: The corridor stretching ahead looked exactly like the one they had just left behind. It was long and narrow, illuminated by torches, and weapons were hanging on the walls everywhere. For about forty meters until the next corner, which might be a good distance away, there wasn’t even a hint of an exit to be found.
