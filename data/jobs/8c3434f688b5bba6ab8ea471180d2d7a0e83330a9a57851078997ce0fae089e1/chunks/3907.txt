Laura stepped into the dark stairwell and illuminated it with the torch above her head. The snail-shell spiral seemed endless as it ascended, disappearing into darkness at its top. Laura nudged Kaja, who stood beside her with a frightened look on her face. "Come," she said, placing one foot on the lowest step and began the long climb upward. Kaja followed her. After a few steps, Laura paused once more and turned to the janitor. "Thank you. Many thanks."
