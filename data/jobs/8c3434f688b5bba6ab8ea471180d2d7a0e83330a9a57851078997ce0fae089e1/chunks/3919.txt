Lukas nodded. As he opened his wardrobe and fumbled with the secret compartment inside, he told the girls about his meeting with Attila and the initial mistrust he had shown him. This mistrust only grew when the headmaster urgently advised him not to inform their stepmother about Laura and Kaja's disappearance. He had claimed that it would lead nowhere and promised to look for the girls himself. If there were anyone who could find them, then he was the one. No one would know Ravenstein Castle better than him – because he had been doing his job for more than a hundred years!
