In the bright moonlight, they were clearly visible from afar. That's why they moved very close to the walls of the castle as they crept towards the park. Suddenly, they jumped in surprise. An unknown figure was approaching them. It was a man. As much as possible, they pressed themselves into the shadows of the wall and held their breath while the stranger drew near. Fortunately, it was only Mr. Schnuffelpuff, the history teacher, who was aimlessly heading towards the school building and paid them no attention at all. After he had disappeared inside the castle, the three waited a few more seconds out of caution before continuing on their way.
