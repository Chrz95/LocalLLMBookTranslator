The friends turned down the path that meandered towards Professor Morgenstern's house. Winged black shadows ghosted through the night. Laura looked up at the sky and thought she could see the stone curlew pair nesting in the old oak behind the gymnasium. The eerie calls it made shortly after confirmed her suspicion. Then, suddenly, a swarm of bats silently flew over their heads from the eastern tower. It almost seemed as if these fluttering creatures were offering escort to the friends on their important mission.
