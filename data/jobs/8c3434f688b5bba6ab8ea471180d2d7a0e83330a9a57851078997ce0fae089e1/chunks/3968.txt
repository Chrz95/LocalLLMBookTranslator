The attack came so unexpectedly that Laura was too late to react. Only at the very last moment did she throw herself to the side. By a hair's breadth, she avoided the sharp claws, but lost her balance and stumbled. To prevent a fall, she had to paddle with her arms, and the cup slipped from her hands. It fell to the ground and tipped over. With a loud clatter, the lid came off, and the precious water of life began to pour out.
