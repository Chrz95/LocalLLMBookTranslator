With eyes wide with fright, Albin Ellerking stared at the cat, motionless and apparently unable to comprehend what had befallen his beloved pet. It must have been something terrible, for the gardener finally closed his eyes. Then Laura could see it too: The feline was rotting alive! As if a corrosive acid were dissolving it from within, large holes rapidly ate their way into its rust-brown fur, and the tissue beneath had already transformed into putrid flesh emitting an nauseating stench of carrion.
