Albin Ellerking howled in pain, and for the first time his friends could hear his voice. It was thin and squeaky like that of an old, feeble creature. "No! No!" the gardener screamed madly, dashed to his cat, cradled it in his arms, and fled into the darkness of night. The crows in the sky followed them. The enormous flock turned away and vanished instantly without a trace.
