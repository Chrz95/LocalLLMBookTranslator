Laura Leander has her head full of worries. A year ago, her father Marius disappeared without a trace. Her perpetually nagging stepmother makes her life miserable. At school, Laura faces two Fives on the semester report card. She has indeed grown very close to her younger brother Lukas, but his constant know-it-all attitude really gets under her skin. And then there are these gloomy dreams and eerie incidents. During a horseback ride with her horse Stormwind, Laura is attacked by crows and a dark rider pursues her. Or is she just imagining this as some cruel prank? On her thirteenth birthday, her life is turned upside down entirely. For Laura has been chosen to be the Guardian of Light, to free the Cup of Enlightenment from the hands of the Dark Powers. Only in this way can she prevent Borboron, the Black Prince of Darkness on Planet Aventerra, from finally seizing power there. If that happens, the fate of Aventerra and humanity would be sealed. Both would fall into the Eternal Nothingness. An bitter struggle begins, where evil seems to always have one step ahead...
