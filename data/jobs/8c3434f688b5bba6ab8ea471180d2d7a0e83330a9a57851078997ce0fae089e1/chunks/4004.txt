For a moment only, the friends felt a sense of dread as they arrived at the Old Crypt. They waited expectantly for the shrill squawking of the ravens to greet them. But in the treetops above, all was silent. When Laura took a closer look at the tree crowns, she could no longer spot even one mistletoe bush. Yet there had been so many! Laura suspected that now the dead birds were attending their master and lord. Since Albin Ellerking was currently occupied with his dark arts to keep grudges alive, it seemed quite obvious to him that the ravens did not guard the crypt as they should have done. The friends therefore reached the entrance of the crypt without any hindrance.
