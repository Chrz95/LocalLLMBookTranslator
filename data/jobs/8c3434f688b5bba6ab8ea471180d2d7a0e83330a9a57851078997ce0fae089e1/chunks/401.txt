A few minutes later, they had left the houses of Hohenstadt behind them. The road meandered through the dreamy hilly landscape, crossed brooks and rivers, wound through valleys, and climbed gentle inclines. Laura knew the route from Hohenstadt to Ravenstein by heart. She no longer needed to look out the window to know where they were at any given moment. How many times had she driven this stretch? So many times that she could hardly count them anymore.
