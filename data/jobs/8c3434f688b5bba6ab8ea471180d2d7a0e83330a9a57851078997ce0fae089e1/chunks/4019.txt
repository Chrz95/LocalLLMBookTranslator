Finally, Laura and Kaja had reached the tomb chamber. The secret door that had caused them so much trouble on their first visit stood wide open. Albin Ellerking must have forgotten to close it when he had hurried after them earlier. Laura and Kaja entered the chamber and stopped in front of the small altar in the wall. Without a word, they looked up at the cup of enlightenment that gleamed in the light from their flashlights as if bathed by the brightest light.
