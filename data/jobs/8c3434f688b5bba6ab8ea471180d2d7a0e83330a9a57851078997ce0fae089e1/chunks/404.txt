Now and then Sayelle cast a surreptitious glance in the rearview mirror to observe the children. But they seemed not to notice it at all. Laura's face was as pale as chalk and she stared blankly ahead. Lukas played wordlessly with his old tennis ball. He tossed it up with his right hand, catching it again. He repeated this procedure tirelessly: ten times, fifty times, a hundred times. This damned tic was absolutely infuriating, thought Sayelle.
