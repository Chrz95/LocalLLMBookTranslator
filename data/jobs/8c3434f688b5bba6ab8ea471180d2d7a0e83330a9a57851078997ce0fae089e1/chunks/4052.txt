Paralyzed she stood there and stared at the swelling floodwaters. Without Laura being able to resist it, long suppressed images rose up within her. Images of a car accident in which she and her mother had been trapped inside. Back then too, the water had risen higher and higher, higher and higher, until... Laura felt an endless panic seize hold of her. She was unable to form a clear thought, unable to move. With wide-open eyes, she stood there and stared apathetically ahead. Only her lips moved as she whispered, "No, no, no!" Over and over again only "No, no!" Then she began to tremble all over and breathe heavily.
