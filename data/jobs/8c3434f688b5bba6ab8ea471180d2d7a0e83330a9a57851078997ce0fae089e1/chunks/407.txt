The felt covering was already almost worn off, and that it had once been canary-yellow could no longer be recognized. Well, whatever. For Lucas, the tennis ball was a precious possession. Almost sacred. Because it was the ball with which Boris Becker had turned his deciding matchball into victory at his first Wimbledon win. Not as if Boris Becker had meant anything to Lucas in any way. Not at all. After all, he hadn't even been born yet then. He knew that Boris had once been a great tennis player, but he himself had never seen him play. The reason why this tennis ball was so overwhelmingly important for Lucas was solely his father.
