Lukas searched the wall for the Templar seal with great haste. He spotted the stone on which it was engraved rather quickly and placed his thumb upon it, pressing with all his might. Yet even though he did everything just as his sister had done last time, nothing happened this time around. The door did not budge an inch to the side. Again, the boy pressed the seal - again without success. The opening mechanism was no longer functioning. Was it jammed? Or perhaps it had been rendered inoperative in some other way? Be that as it may - Lukas suddenly felt that the girls were in great danger.
