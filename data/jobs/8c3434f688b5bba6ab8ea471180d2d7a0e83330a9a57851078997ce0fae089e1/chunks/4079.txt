The water in the tomb chamber already stood nearly a meter high. Laura knelt on the sarcophagus of Reimar von Ravenstein. She had Kaja, who had regained consciousness and was struggling to sit up, under her arms and tried hard to pull her onto the tombstone. The cup of enlightenment she had already placed there. On the large sarcophagus they were safe from the rapidly rising waters for at least a while - and perhaps this time would be enough so that Lukas could free them. Provided, of course, that she had actually managed to give him a sign. Whether her limited telekinetic abilities had been sufficient?
