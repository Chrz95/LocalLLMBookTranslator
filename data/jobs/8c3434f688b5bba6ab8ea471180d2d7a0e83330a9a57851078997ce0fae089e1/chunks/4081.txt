"Ja... ja," mumbled Kaja drowsily. That she was already up to her waist in the icy water seemed not to have occurred to her yet. But at last Laura's encouragement had paid off. The little girl clung to Reimar's resting place, pushed and pulled herself upward with all her might until finally, with Laura's help, she climbed onto the sarcophagus lid. Exhausted, Kaja slumped down, and even Laura struggled for breath.
