The usually mild-mannered giant looked tense. The door was extremely sturdy, and the attempt to break it down seemed to be overwhelming even him. Portak spat in his fists, grabbed the tree trunk again, and rammed it with all his might against the wall. A stone block loosened, mortar chunks and dust fell out of the joints, and on the next strike the stone already wobbled visibly. The column giant doubled his efforts. Now like a steam engine the trunk fell down onto the loosened door frame, and more and more stones rattled under the untamed force of his blows.
