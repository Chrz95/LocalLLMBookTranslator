The flood hit Lukas with the force of a giant fist. It swept him off his feet and dragged him along. Lukas desperately searched for something to hold onto, but his hands grasped at nothing. He tumbled in the churning waters and was carried farther away. The air grew scarce, but as he frantically tried to catch his breath, he choked and filled his lungs with water. He gasped and spat out, thinking he would drown until he felt solid ground beneath him again. A few meters more, he scuffed on the bare stone floor at the back of his legs, then the ordeal was over. The flood had receded to a ankle-deep stream, and Lukas cautiously pulled himself up.
