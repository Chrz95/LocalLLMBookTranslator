The horse hooves drummed out a staccato on the hard-frozen ground as the two riders galloped through the Hangman's Wood. The trees whizzed past them like blurred shadows. Laura held the reins with only one hand, for in her left arm she clutched the cup of illumination, carefully ensuring it remained upright to avoid spilling its precious contents. She was glad that despite the infernal pace, she managed to stay in the saddle and thanked her father for teaching her to ride from a very young age.
