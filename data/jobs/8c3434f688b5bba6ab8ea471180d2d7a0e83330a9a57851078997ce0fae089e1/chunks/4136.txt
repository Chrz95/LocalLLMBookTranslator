Gradually, the trees began to thin out, and Laura and Percy reached the edge of the forest. Before them lay Ravenstein Park. Without stopping, they continued galloping forward. Suddenly, Laura spotted four riders: three black riders on black horses and a gray rider on a steely-gray horse riding at her left flank. From some distance away, they dashed out from behind a group of bushes positioned diagonally in front of them and angled towards them at an acute angle. It was clear that they intended to block their path to the lake.
