Paravain felt that his despair was growing. Was it all coming to an end now? Had all his striving and endeavor been in vain? Since he had been called at the age of thirteen to Grail Castle, where he received his training as a knight, he had fought for the cause of light. In countless battles and skirmishes, he had stood against the Black Powers. And time after time, it had succeeded him and his men to repel and drive back the attacks of the Dark Armies. Should this now all have been in vain?
