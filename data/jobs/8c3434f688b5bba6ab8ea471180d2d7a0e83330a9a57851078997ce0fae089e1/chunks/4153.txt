Just before they reached the shore of the lake, Laura closed her eyes. She felt as if a strong gust of wind had leapt from the ground - and in that moment she saw the light. A stormy roar filled Laura, and around her was only a blinding whirlwind. Everything was now dazzlingly bright, and she knew herself one with the light. Every sense of gravity and any feeling for space and time had fallen away from her. Bodyless, she floated through the endless light.
