Laura looked around and saw the trail that had formed through the underbrush. The thorny branches and twigs had bent to one side, and a narrow path led straight ahead towards the center of the island. There, above the heart of the isle, rose a radiant beam of light that seemed to stretch into infinity. In the middle of the light stood a knight in white armor. He appeared to be waiting for Laura, as he waved at her and indicated that she should hurry up.
