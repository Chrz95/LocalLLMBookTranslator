Laura leaped from the saddle and hurried with the chalice to the light pillar. But as her feet touched the ground, the sun rose on the horizon. The night of the winter solstice was over. With the first ray of sunlight, the light pillar faded away; the luminous mist dome above the island dissipated, the branches of the bushes returned to their old position, so that the path disappeared and the underbrush became as impenetrable again as before.
