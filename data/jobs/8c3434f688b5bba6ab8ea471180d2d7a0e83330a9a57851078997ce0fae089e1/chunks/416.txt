Paravain made himself guilty, and the bitter feeling of guilt gnawed at him. He and no one else was responsible for his lord's well-being. Even though he had carried out his duty without flaw or fault over all those years—now he had failed. The unfathomable had happened: Pestilenz, the sword of evil, had inflicted a wound upon the guardian of light—and he, the leader of the bodyguard, could not prevent it. And what was even worse—
