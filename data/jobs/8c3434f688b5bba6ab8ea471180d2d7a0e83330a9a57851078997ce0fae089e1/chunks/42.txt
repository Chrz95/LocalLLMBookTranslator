At first, Laura could not remember anything. She looked around her room as if she might find the answer there. It was hardly different from a room of other girls her age. On the walls hung pictures of horses and posters of her favorite bands. Next to her wardrobe stood a large bookshelf with her books: "The Infinite Story," "The Golden Compass," and other thick novels. Opposite the bed, on the wall, stood her desk, and above it hung a movie poster from which Frodo Beutlin stared at her with his big brown eyes.
