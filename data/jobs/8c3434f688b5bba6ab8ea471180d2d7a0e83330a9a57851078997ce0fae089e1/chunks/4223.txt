Elysion opened the window. Fresh and clear air streamed into the chamber and drove away the stench of death. The old man raised his head to meet the winter sun, closed his eyes, and drew in the fragrant scent rising from the whispering grass plain. After he had filled himself with this aroma, he opened his eyes again and let his gaze wander over the walls of Hellunyat and the surroundings of the Grail Castle.
