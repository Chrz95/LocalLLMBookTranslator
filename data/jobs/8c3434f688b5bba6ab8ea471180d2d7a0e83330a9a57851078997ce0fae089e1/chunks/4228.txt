Morwena and Ritter Paravain stepped beside their lord, and together they gazed out into the brightening day, at the new life in Aventerra. Their eyes roamed freely to the horizon, from the Modermoor in the east, through the Drachenbergs in the south, and across the Raunewald in the west. And even though it was early morning, they could still see one of the two moons of Aventerra in the sky.
