At the very moment when she was most urgently needed, the healer from Hellunyat paid a visit to her father Rumor, the king of the distant Nebellands. A few days ago, he had sent an envoy to her and requested it. Morwena was the only one who might be able to help Elysion. Without question, she set out immediately in order to return as quickly as possible to Hellunyat after Pfeilschwinge delivered Paravain’s message. Even on the fastest mount, she would need several days for the journey. Moreover, the Black Powers would do everything within their power to delay or even prevent Morwena’s return. It was quite likely that she would arrive too late...
