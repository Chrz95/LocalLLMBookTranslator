On the desk reigned a chaotic mess: books, notebooks, and magazines were scattered haphazardly across it. In between lay CDs and floppy disks, and a colorful assortment of ballpoint pens, markers, pencil tips, and erasers completed the disarray. Some framed photos that usually lined up there had fallen over and not been put back in place again. When Laura's gaze fell on one of the photos showing her in her white fencing uniform with foil saber in hand, she suddenly remembered something.
