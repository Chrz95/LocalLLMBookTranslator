Reimar von Ravenstein, a notorious robber knight, had built the castle in the first half of the twelfth century. It served as his residence and stronghold from which he wreaked havoc and terrorized the surrounding area. His cruelty towards his enemies was legendary, just as infamous were his callous treatment of his subjects. Taxes and levies were exorbitantly high. He squeezed every last penny out of the poor people who lived in dire straits. Yet anyone who rebelled against his tyrannical rule was mercilessly punished. A single false word could mean death, and a mere disapproving glance had sent countless individuals to prison, where they faced a long and torturous demise.
