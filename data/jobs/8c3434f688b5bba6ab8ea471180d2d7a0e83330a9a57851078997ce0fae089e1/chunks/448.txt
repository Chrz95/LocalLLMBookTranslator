Over the centuries, Burg Ravenstein had been destroyed and rebuilt several times. Since 1888, the castle housed the eponymous boarding school, which of course necessitated renovations and expansions. The rooms that once belonged to knights were now classrooms, the chambers where servants lived accommodated students instead, and the knight's hall served as a dining hall. In the outbuildings, former stables, storage rooms, and garden huts, teachers' accommodations had been set up. Only the gymnasium in the park was newly constructed just a few years ago.
