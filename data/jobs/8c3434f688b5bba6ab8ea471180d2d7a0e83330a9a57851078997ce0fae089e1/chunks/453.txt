As Laura shut the car door behind her, she suddenly heard a deep growling in her back. It seemed to come from the depths of the throat and sounded very threatening. Laura flinched. If there was one thing she couldn't stand about death, it were sharp dogs. As a small child, she had been bitten by a dog. The wound hadn't been particularly severe, but since then, Laura had been afraid of dogs. Nervously, she turned around. But there was nothing to be seen; far and wide, no dog could be spotted.
