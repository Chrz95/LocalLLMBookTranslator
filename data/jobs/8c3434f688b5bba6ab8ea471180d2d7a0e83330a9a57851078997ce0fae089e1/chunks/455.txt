The letter trees stood in the middle of the vast lawn that led to the driveway and stretched all the way up to a small group of trees and bushes. They were the favorites of Albin Ellerking, the internat gardener. It hardly ever passed a day when this knobbly man did not take care of his artistic creations, which he had given form and shape many years ago. Almost every day he was at work with his large hedge trimmer, cutting off a millimeter here, trimming a few leaves there or snipping off an overhanging twig so that his wonderful bushes always maintained the perfect shape of two enormous dogs.
