At first glance, he differed little from other men of his age – perhaps in the middle forties. Good: He had a humpbacked nose, and his oversized ears tapered strangely pointy. And deep green eyes were not exactly common either. But still, Albin Ellerking appeared quite harmless. He looked like someone who could never do any harm to even a fly. But that was only at first glance. Although Laura couldn’t explain why, the wordless man had something about him that made her want to avoid him as much as possible.
