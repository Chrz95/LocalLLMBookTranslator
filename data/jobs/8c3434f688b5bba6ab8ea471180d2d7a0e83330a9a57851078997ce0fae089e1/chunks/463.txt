Groll was the rust-brown cat of the gardener and in Laura's eyes as eerie as his master himself. The creature had only one eye left. The other had been knocked out years ago during a fight with a quarrelsome rival for the affection of a feline lady. Groll was pampered to the hilt by Albin Ellerking, and he was now so fat that he could barely walk properly anymore. Most often, the animal perched on his shoulder – like a witch's cat in a fairy tale. But now Groll scurried between Albin’s large feet.
