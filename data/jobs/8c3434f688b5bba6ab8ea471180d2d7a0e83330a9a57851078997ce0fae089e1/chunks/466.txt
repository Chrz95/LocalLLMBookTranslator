Albin Ellerking was indeed known for the fact that he mercilessly reported to the school administration any student who violated the house or park regulations or got into trouble in other ways. Fortunately, this only rarely had consequences because Director Aurelius Morgenstern himself was no friend of rigid rules and therefore usually let mercy prevail over justice in most cases. Nevertheless, none of the students could stand Albin Ellerking. He was almost equally feared by them as Attila Morduk, the housemaster.
