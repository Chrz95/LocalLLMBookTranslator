It was a special column. Whatever had prompted the unknown stonemason who had created it many years ago - he had given her the form of a mighty giant. Although his body was only partially indicated and did not necessarily correspond to human proportions, she still clearly represented a giant. However, the stonemason had paid particular attention to the face with its friendly eyes and the mischievously smiling mouth. With his warm facial features, despite being over five meters tall, he did not appear threatening at all. On the contrary: for Laura, he had something of an affable doorman about him who warmly welcomed students and guests to Burg Ravenstein.
