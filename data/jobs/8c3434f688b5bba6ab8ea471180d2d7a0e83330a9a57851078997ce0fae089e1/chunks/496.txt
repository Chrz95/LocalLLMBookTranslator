The entrance hall, where the corridors led to the classrooms and stairs to other floors, was dark. The narrow windows let in little light; the ceiling lights were not turned on, and naturally, neither were the electric candles of the large Christmas tree that stood in the middle of the hall. Attila Morduk, the caretaker, was very careful about saving money. Therefore, the corridors were not lit during class hours. No one could be seen. As Laura and Lukas walked through the hall, their footsteps echoed off the stone floor tiles, and a biting smell of tile polish filled their noses.
