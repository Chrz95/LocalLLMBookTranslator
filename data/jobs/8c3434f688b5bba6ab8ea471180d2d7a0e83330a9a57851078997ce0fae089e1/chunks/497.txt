Directly across from the entrance portal hung a massive oil painting. It depicted a beautiful young woman in a long white gown; at her feet lay a mighty black wolf. The woman's face was pale, and she looked sad. Utterly sad, as if nothing on earth could lift her spirits. Laura had heard from older students that the woman on the painting was named Silva and had lived during the times of the Cruel Knight Ravenstein. Allegedly, she had a terrible fate, which would explain her deep sadness.
