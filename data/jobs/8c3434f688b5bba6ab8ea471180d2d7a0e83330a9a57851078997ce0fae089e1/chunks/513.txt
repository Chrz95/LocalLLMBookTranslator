When Marius Leander came to himself again, the pains were immediately back. His back burned hellishly, and over his cheeks it seemed as if narrow rivulets of glowing lava were flowing. The cruel lashes from one of the prison guards in the last night had torn his skin, disfigured his face, and transformed his back into a single flaming wound. He could count himself lucky that a compassionate maid had taken pity on him. After the jailer finally released him and left him to his fate, she crept into his soiled cell and took care of his injuries. She had stopped the bleeding, cleaned the wounds, and then applied an odd-looking paste to them. Upon asking her about it, she had whispered that it was a mixture of healing earth and various herbs meant to prevent infection. For a wound fire would mean certain death. That was all he understood before he lost consciousness again.
