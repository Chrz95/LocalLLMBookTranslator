For nearly a year now, he had been held captive by the Dark Powers deep within this windowless dungeon beneath the Dark Fortress. The low-ceilinged room was damp and filthy, teeming with vermin. Fleas, bedbugs, cockroaches, and spiders kept him company during the day, while rats and mice were his companions at night. With the wounds inflicted by the sadistic guard, infection seemed inevitable. Marius Leander could only hope that the mysterious mixture would prove effective. But it was probably irrelevant anyway. Even if he managed to survive the blows, he would likely rot alive in this dungeon.
