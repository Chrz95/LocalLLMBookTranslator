Marius let out another loud moan. He had no idea how long he had been unconscious. In his cell, a dim twilight always prevailed, so that he had lost all sense of time a long time ago. Even the sulfurous smell, which at first had often caused an almost uncontrollable nausea and strong dizziness in him, he hardly noticed anymore. Only the unbearable damp heat of the dungeon still bothered him. He probably would never get used to it. Streams of sweat flowed over his face, and a tormenting pressure weighed on his chest. With great effort, he gasped for air. The tongue in his mouth felt like an old, matted sock. He was thirsty, terribly thirsty. Hopefully there was still water in the jug that stood on the rough wooden table near the bars.
