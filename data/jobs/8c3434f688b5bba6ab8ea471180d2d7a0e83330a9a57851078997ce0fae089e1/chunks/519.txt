Aching, Marius Leander struggled out of his bed, which was covered with musty straw. At first, it seemed as if every part of his body were a single piercing pain. Every fiber burned like the purest infernal fire. After he had managed to take the first step, the pain subsided somewhat. He set one foot in front of the other very cautiously and dragged himself laboriously towards the wooden table. His bare feet made tapping sounds on the rough stone floor; a spider crawled over his right ankle, and a mouse scurried excitedly around and disappeared into its hole.
