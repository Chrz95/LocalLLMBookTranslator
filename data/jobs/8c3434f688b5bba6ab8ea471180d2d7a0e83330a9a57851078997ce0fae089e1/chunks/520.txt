From the corridor, the cell was separated from the rest by a narrow iron grille of thick bars. The door within it was secured with two locks. There was no chance of escape - especially since Marius Leander was under constant supervision. A flickering torch burned in a holder on the wall before the cell. It illuminated the corridor and cast a dim light over most of the cell as well. Underneath the torch stood a small table with two wooden benches, where usually both prison guards sat. But now only one of the coarse-looking fellows was dozing off at the table.
