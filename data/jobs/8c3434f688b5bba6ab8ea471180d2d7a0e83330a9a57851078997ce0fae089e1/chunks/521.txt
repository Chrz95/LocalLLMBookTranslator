His companion, who had pulled his left leg slightly, probably inspected the other cells and made sure everything was in order and all prisoners were securely held. Marius Leander could only guess about this because he didn't know if there were any other prisoners at all or how many. The neighboring cells were certainly empty; he never heard a sound from them. As for what it looked like in the further away dungeons, he had no idea. He did remember hearing the terrifying screams of a man one night, but that was already long ago. Since then, there had been silence.
