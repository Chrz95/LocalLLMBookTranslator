The guard raised his head when he sensed movement in the cell and glared at the prisoner for a moment with his three eyes suspiciously. Like his companion, he was also a Trioktide. He had an additional third eye right in the middle of his forehead. This third eye was always awake whenever the normal pair remained closed, allowing the guard to keep an eye on the prisoner even during sleep. For this reason, the Trioktides were primarily used by the Dark Powers as guards or scouts during military campaigns. Marius had been terrified when he first encountered a Trioktide but had now grown accustomed to their appearance.
