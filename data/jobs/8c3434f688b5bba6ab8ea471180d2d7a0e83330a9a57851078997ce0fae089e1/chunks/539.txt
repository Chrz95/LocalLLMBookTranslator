The first was a tall man. Although he wore a long black cloak, it was impossible to miss his extremely strong and muscular build. Moreover, he had the determined strides of a man accustomed to overcoming every obstacle in his path. The torch in his hand illuminated not only the way but also cast his face into a bright light. His face with its hooked nose and thin-lipped mouth was pale as death, yet his eyes glowed red. Marius Leander had never met this man before. But no sooner did he see the enormous sword hanging at his side than he knew who it belonged to: Borboron, the Black Prince - the powerful leader of the Dark Armies.
