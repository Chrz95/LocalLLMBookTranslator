Aura lived on the third floor of the main building. The room was small but quite cozy and like most rooms at Ravenstein, it was furnished for two occupants. To the left and right of the door stood beds, wardrobes, and shelves, while in front of the window a large tabletop served as a desk. Two chairs were placed before it. Film posters, posters of film stars and pop stars, and photos of whales and dolphins covered the free spaces on the walls.
