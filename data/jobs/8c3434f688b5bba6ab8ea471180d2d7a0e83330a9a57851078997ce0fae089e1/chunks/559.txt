Laura cast her friend a worried glance. It was clear that even the slightest movement had left her out of breath. Kaja sat exactly under a humpback whale poster, and above her head spun a funny whale mobile – Kaja was indeed a whale freak. Some from their class claimed she must inevitably be fascinated by these imposing sea creatures due to her ample figure. Laura found this not only mean-spirited but also grossly exaggerated. Kaja had absolutely nothing in common with a whale, as these scoundrels maintained – at most just a tiny, teeny-tiny bit.
