The next day was a Sunday – the second Advent. A pale-blue winter sky stretched over Hohenstadt, where a feeble sun hung. The town possessed almost an intact medieval core. Its winding streets and dreamy alleys with lovingly restored historical buildings drew many tourists in summer, bustling from early morning until late evening. In winter it was only slightly quieter because even the traditional Christmas market around the majestic Christmas tree on the town hall square attracted a crowd of visitors, especially on weekends.
