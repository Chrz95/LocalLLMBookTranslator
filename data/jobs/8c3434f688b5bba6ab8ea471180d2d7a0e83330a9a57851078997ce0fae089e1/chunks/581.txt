The Black Prince and his companions had arrived in front of the cell, and Marius could now also make out the other two figures. To his surprise, one of them was a tall woman. The emerald-green dress that clung closely to her body gave her an almost feral appearance. Her eyes with slit-like pupils in the yellow iris reminded him of reptiles as well. Her face, framed by pitch-black hair, was pale and rigid, showing no signs of emotion.
