The reptilian woman with her deathly pale face observed him with a cold gaze. A mocking gleam appeared in her yellow reptilian eyes. "Don't worry too much," she sneered. "Even with the chain, your daughter would not have been able to fulfill her task. Never!" She stepped very close to Marius, pushed out her sharp chin, and stared him directly in the eyes. As she continued speaking, her voice had a growling sound of a wild cat. "The wheel of time is much better safeguarded in my hands than with your little fool and will be of incalculable benefit to me!"
