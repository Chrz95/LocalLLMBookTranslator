Of course - of course he had heard about this terrible torture. It was worse than the worst death. Now Marius Leander also understood why the Fhurhur had come along with him. Only the Fhurhurs were capable of affixing a person to death's grip. Although Marius didn't know how they managed it, he knew all too well what result this procedure had: A person in death's grip was no longer able to move. Not an inch, not a muscle, not even the smallest fiber of their body. Yet he remained fully conscious and his mind stayed sharp. To outsiders, it must have seemed as if the affected person lay in a death-like coma. A terrible fate that even death could not put an end to. For those in death's grip never die. If they are not promptly freed by an antidote, their alert mind remains imprisoned within their rigid body until the end of all time. But this antidote was also known only to the Fhurhurs.
