They tasted of nothing. Not a thing at all. After a few seconds, Marius felt a pleasant warmth spreading through his body - and suddenly he could no longer move. He wanted to raise an arm, but the command from his brain simply did not execute it. His legs were just as paralyzed. All limbs were immobilized; not even the slightest movement was possible anymore. Although Marius had clear consciousness, his body had turned to stone. However, the unbearable pain had disappeared. But Marius could only briefly rejoice in this because the thought that ran through his fully awake mind hurt more than any of the worst physical torments: He could no longer help Laura now!
