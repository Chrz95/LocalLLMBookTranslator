She pressed the doorknob cautiously down, slowly opened the door and glanced into the room - it was empty. Frau Pieselstein, the internate secretary, was not in her place. Her desk chair was unoccupied. Of course, Frau Pieselstein did not go by that name. Her real name was Frau Priese-Stein. But already a day after she had started working as the secretary, the Ravensteins had given her the nickname.
