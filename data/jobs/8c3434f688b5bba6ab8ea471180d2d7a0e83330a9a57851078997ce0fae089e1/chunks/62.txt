When Laura looked out the window into her garden after dressing up, she thought for a moment that it had finally snowed. But then, in the next instant, she realized her mistake and felt disappointed. She had hoped so much that there would be snow on her birthday this year as well – just like last year when it started snowing on December fourth, and by Laura's birthday on the fifth of December, the whole country was covered in a thick blanket of snow. Laura and Lukas had spent the entire day playing outside with their friends, sledding and snowboarding, and they had even organized a fun snowball fight. But later that afternoon, it reached its peak: her father Marius had arranged an old horse-drawn sled, harnessed Laura's horse Stormwind to it, and then the whole gang had gone on a sled ride through meadows and forests. Laura still heard the gentle jingling of the bells Marius had attached to Stormwind’s harness as she felt the muted hoofbeats against her ear and the snow crunched under the sled wheels. She had felt like she was in a winter fairytale, with only one thing missing: that an ice giant would come around the corner or invite the Snow Princess into her icy palace. But even though nothing quite like that happened, Laura would never forget this day – not for the rest of her life. For just two weeks later, three days before Christmas, her father Marius had suddenly disappeared.
