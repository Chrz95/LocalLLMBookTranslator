Dr. Quintus Schwartz was approaching fifty but looked younger than his age. His facial features resembled those of the Roman emperors that Laura had learned about from history books. Dr. Schwartz was always lightly tanned, and his dark hair, which still showed not a single hint of gray, was always stylishly cut and meticulously combed. Quintus Schwartz would have had an entirely likable appearance if it weren't for his piercing dark eyes, which seemed to bore into the person opposite him with relentless intensity.
