After dinner one evening, a colleague had observed Marius Leander leaving the boarding school building. From that point on, he vanished into thin air without a trace. Although his family naturally contacted the police and conducted their own inquiries, there was not even the slightest clue about what had happened to him until today. No one knew where Marius could have gone. He seemed to have disappeared from under the earth, and all—police, colleagues, friends, and of course especially his family—were left with a mystery.
