The most striking thing about him were his arms. They were so long that they almost reached to the knees. Moreover, they were so heavily covered in hair that it looked as if they were clad in a thick, rust-brown fur that also grew vigorously on his hand palms. The hands were disproportionately large. Not quite as big as toilet lids, as some students claimed, but still so enormous that Attila could effortlessly lift a medicine ball with one hand.
