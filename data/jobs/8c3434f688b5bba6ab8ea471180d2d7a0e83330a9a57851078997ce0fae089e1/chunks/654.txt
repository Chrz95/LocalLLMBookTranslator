Attila Morduk had been the housemaster at Ravenstein for what seemed like an eternity, and he had been a terror to students just as long. He always wore a fearsome grimace on his face. Not one student could remember Attila Morduk ever having laughed or even smiled. He looked perpetually gloomy, as if he wanted to compete with the darkest heavy-metal band.

Most boys and girls got scared at the mere sight of him and made sure to avoid him.
