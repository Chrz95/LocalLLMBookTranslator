Orwena urged her horse with relentless force. "Keep going, Fairy Bride, keep running," she shouted against the howling wind. Fairy Bride lifted her head; the two slender ivory horns on her forehead gleamed in the sunlight. She snorted briefly and then struggled forward through the snow that almost reached her belly. Beneath the thick blanket of snow, the narrow path leading up to the pass was barely visible. Moreover, it climbed so steeply that it would have been impassable for nearly any horse. Fairy Bride, Orwena's two-horned steed, however, caused no difficulty at all. She effortlessly conquered the ascent and none of her steps missed their mark.
