Two-horned creatures were not only more robust than horses but also much more reliable than their shy relatives, the delicate unicorns, which had become rare and could be found almost exclusively in the fairy forests. Yet two-horned creatures were almost as gentle and sensitive as they were. They sniffed out dangers well ahead of time and could therefore avoid them in good time. For this reason, Morwena had chosen a two-horned creature for her mount, and Fairy Bride had never let her down before. Two-horned creatures knew the ancient paths that crisscrossed the lands of Aventerra and brought the Wise to their destination much faster than other beings could manage. After all, these secret paths were laid by fairies who took care of both unicorns and two-horned creatures during their first time after birth.
