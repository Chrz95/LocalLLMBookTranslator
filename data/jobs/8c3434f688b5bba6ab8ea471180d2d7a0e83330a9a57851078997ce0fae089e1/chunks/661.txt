The thought of the trick with which they had lured her out of the Holy Grail Castle infuriated Morwena. She had not even suspected anything when the supposed messenger of her father appeared. On the contrary, his message had been a most welcome occasion for her to finally return home after years of absence. It was only when her father surprisedly greeted her that she realized something was amiss. Shortly thereafter, Pfeilschwinge, the mighty eagle and messenger of Light, appeared in the sky over Rumorrögk, the castle of her father. He had informed her about what had happened on Hellunyat during her absence. Naturally, she set out immediately again. Her father's offer to provide her with some riders for protection was declined by her. A larger group would have moved slower and attracted attention. Furthermore, only two-horned creatures could follow the secret paths.
