Finally, Morwena had reached the pass. She let the two-horned beast pant and cast a glance into the valley where the green expanses of the marshlands stretched out before her. The healer sighed. The road to Hellunyat was still infinitely long. She would not only have to cross the marshlands and conquer the raging Donnerfluss, but also face the terrifying Glimmerwaste after that, which was probably the most dangerous part of the journey. And finally, she had to make it through the Dark Chasm safely as well.
