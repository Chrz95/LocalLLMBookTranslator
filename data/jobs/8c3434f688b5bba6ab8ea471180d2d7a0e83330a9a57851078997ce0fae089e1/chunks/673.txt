Of course Max wasn't Stinkefurz but Maximilian Finkensturz. His terrible habit of spreading disgusting odors everywhere had quickly earned him the nickname "Stinkefurz" despite his ungainly appearance. Max Stinkefurz couldn't stand Laura because Ronnie Riedel didn't like her either. Ronnie was Max's best buddy and had been class president last year. At the start of the new school year, however – with a large majority and completely unexpectedly for Ronnie – his classmates had voted in favor of Laura. This deeply affected Ronnie, especially since Laura was newly transferred to 7b, and ever since then he only caused her stress. Naturally, Ronnie also belonged to those few who didn't congratulate her on her birthday.
