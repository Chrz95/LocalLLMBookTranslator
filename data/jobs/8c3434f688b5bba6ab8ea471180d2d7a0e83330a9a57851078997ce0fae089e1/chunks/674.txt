Laura could not quite concentrate on the lesson properly. She kept thinking about Professor Morgenstern. Oddly enough, he had suddenly fallen ill. On Friday she still had a class with him and then it seemed that he was in perfect physical condition. Of course, the professor was already an older man, probably close to seventy or even older. But Laura could not remember ever having seen him sick before. In fact - Morgenstern always exuded health, and despite his age, he was still capable of making a giant tire swing with one hand, something that few students, including Laura herself, could claim. And now an illness about which the doctor himself did not even know what it was...
