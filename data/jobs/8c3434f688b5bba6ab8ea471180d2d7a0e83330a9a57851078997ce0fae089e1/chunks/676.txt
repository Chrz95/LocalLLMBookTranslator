The teachers to whom Laura asked about Professor Morgenstern's condition after the incident knew nothing more specific either. Even Schnuffelpuff had no inkling of what was going on. With his real name being Dr. Heinrich Schneider-Ruff, he was a history teacher at the internate, an older gentleman with a bald head and an old-fashioned pair of glasses, whose nervous habit of constantly sniffing loudly up his nose had earned him the nickname Schnuffelpuff. Normally, Schnuffelpuff would have been well-informed about events on Ravenstein. But this time, even he was clueless. Laura would therefore need to ask Miss Mary, but unfortunately she only had lessons with her again the next day.
