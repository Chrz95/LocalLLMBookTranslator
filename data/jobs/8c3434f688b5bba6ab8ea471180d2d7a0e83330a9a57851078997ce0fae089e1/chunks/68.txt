At this very moment it noticed the crow. Laura stopped and looked over to the bird perched on a tree in front of her window. To protect against the cold, he had puffed out his pitch-black feathers, making him appear even larger than he already was. Laura had never seen such an enormous crow before. She sat there almost motionless like a black ghost, staring at Laura with dark button eyes. Just as she had read that some ancient tribes considered ravens omens of misfortune. Without thinking, she began to shiver. A chill ran down her spine and goosebumps formed on her forearms.
