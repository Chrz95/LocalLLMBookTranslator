Suddenly, Laura felt as if she were being watched. She could practically feel the gazes directed at her. She paused during her stretching exercises, stood up straight, and turned around. With keen eyesight, she scanned the hall. But there was no one. No matter how hard Laura tried to spot someone, she couldn't find anyone, not even in the dimly lit part of the hall. It was quite clear that she had been mistaken. Shrugging her shoulders, she resumed her exercises and thus could not see the dark figure that now emerged from behind a pile of floor mats at the farthest corner of the hall, watching Laura with a grim look. Immediately after that, the mysterious observer retreated back behind the mats again because another door in the other half of the hall was opened. A young man entered.
