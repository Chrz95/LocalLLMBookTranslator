Laura had to smile. Percy Valiant came from France. Born in Burgundy, his parents had given him the old Breton name "Perceval." However, this was too complicated for relatives and friends, so he had been called Percy since childhood. His German knowledge he had mainly improved through reading his favorite books - medieval verse romances and chivalric epics. Percy was indeed a fervent medievalist. If he could have chosen another time to live than the present day, without a moment's hesitation, he would have chosen the age of knights, minstrels, and fair maidens of the castle. This naturally also explained his choice of reading material. The linguistic peculiarities of these books had left unmistakable traces in his German, which already sounded comical due to his pronounced French accent. Percy knew this, but made no attempt to change it. And that would have been a pity, thought Laura. It sounded funny how Percy spoke, and she simply liked it.
