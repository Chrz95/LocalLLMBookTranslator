Once again, he let his eyes sweep across the other half of the hall. His gaze lingered for several seconds on a pile of mats - but there was nothing. No one could be seen. Percy turned around and took a step towards Laura when suddenly a door slammed shut. The PE teacher spun around, and Laura also stared into the distant corner of the hall where the sound had come from. There, right next to the stacked mats, was a small emergency exit in the wall. It trembled slightly as if it had just been closed.
