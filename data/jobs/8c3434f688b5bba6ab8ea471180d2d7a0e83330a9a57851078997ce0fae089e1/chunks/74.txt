Anna Leander, Laura and Lukas' biological mother, drowned in a tragic car accident when Laura was just five years old. Laura had also been sitting in the car at the time; she was rescued at the last minute. Sayelle Rüchlin, with whom Laura's parents had been friends since their shared student days, had taken care of Laura and Lukas with great tenderness ever since her mother's death and had tried to help them get over the loss of their mother as best they could. Marius naturally owed Sayelle an infinite debt for her loving care, and so it was almost inevitable that the two became closer and eventually married after a while.
