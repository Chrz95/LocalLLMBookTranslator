Marius was happy with the journalist whose career had risen steeply since her marriage. Although she therefore had little time for the family, Laura and Lukas felt well-rounded with her at first. But Sayelle's mysterious disappearance had changed Marius significantly. Not that she no longer took care of the children - quite the opposite: Sayelle supervised them even more strictly from then on. She checked everything and sniffed almost constantly behind their backs. Especially behind Laura. But simultaneously, she behaved dismissively towards them. At first, Lukas and Laura had suspected that her stepmother now also feared for herself. And since Sayelle had to get over the loss of her beloved husband, they took a lot from her. But with time, her stepmother became so bad that Laura would have preferred to argue only with her. But on this morning, she didn't feel like having any more nonsense, and so she just mumbled: "Sorry! Tomorrow."
