In the great hall that had once been the site of extravagant feasts and raucous drinking bouts during the days of the Cruel Knight, it was still bustling now, even though Reimar and his wild companions had lain in the dust of history for over eight hundred years. The high ancient wooden ceiling, the paneled walls made of wood, and the grand wrought-iron chandeliers still evoked memories of the eventful past of the hall, although no wax candles or torches flickered within them anymore but rather ordinary light bulbs provided a cozy glow. The Advent wreath hanging from the ceiling in the middle of the room had real candles on it; two were burning and thin smoke plumes rose from their tips to the ceiling.
