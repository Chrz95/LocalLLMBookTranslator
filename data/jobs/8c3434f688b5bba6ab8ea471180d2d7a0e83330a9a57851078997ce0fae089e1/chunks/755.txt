The teacher's desk stood on a small wooden platform at the front of the hall, so that they could keep an eye on their students well, even though this usually didn't bear fruit. The pupils were hardly disturbed by the presence of the teachers, and the teachers generally allowed them to do as they pleased. Even Professor Aurelius Morgenstern only intervened scoldingly when the students got too wild, which occurred very rarely indeed. Almost never. Morgenstern was firmly convinced that eating should be fun. Not just tasty food was necessary, but also an animated conversation and good mood.
