Laura's table was the third one from the entrance and stood by the window side. She sat directly next to the corridor, beside her Kaja, and across from her were her brother and Magda Schneider, a girl in Laura's class. Magda was about as tall as Laura and also had long blonde hair, but she wasn't nearly as pretty. At least that's what Philipp Boddin claimed, who also went to 7b. His classmates called him Mr. Cool because Philipp always made himself look super cool, always only wore top-notch clothes, and marched off every second week to the hairdresser to have his semiblonde mane styled trendy. And of course, Mr. Cool knew a thing or two about women. He claimed he did at least. Laura found him ridiculous, and most other girls in her class shared this opinion. Except for Caro Thiele, of course. She was hopelessly infatuated with Mr. Cool. But Caro was always a bit off the mark.
