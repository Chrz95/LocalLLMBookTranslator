At this very moment, they were interrupted by the ringing of a bell. Dr. Schwartz had something important to announce. Messages and orders from the school administration were always announced during dinner. Only the respective principal was entitled to make such an announcement unless he explicitly granted someone else in the faculty permission to address the students. Professor Morgenstern had occasionally done this, which is why the students had already heard speeches by various teachers. For example, from Schnuffelpuff or Magister Sebaldus, as the spindly and always somewhat disheveled German teacher Sebald Mages was only called.
