Dr. Quintus Schwartz let the general discontent fall completely on deaf ears. Indifferent, he repeated the most important regulations: "Starting now, meal times, study periods, and rest hours will be strictly adhered to again. Students are only allowed to stay in their designated rooms; entering the library or gym outside of regular opening hours is prohibited. And this is very important: During general night-time rest, which will begin punctually at 10 PM from today onwards, it is strictly forbidden for any student to be out of his dormitory unless he has explicit permission from school authorities!"
