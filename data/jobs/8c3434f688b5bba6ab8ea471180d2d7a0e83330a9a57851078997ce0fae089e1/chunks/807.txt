Laura Leander shook her head. She simply didn't understand what Quintus Schwartz intended with this order, which had no basis at all. The substitute Direx was certainly not popular among the students and hardly had any friends among his colleagues either. But even his worst enemies wouldn't deny that he was extremely intelligent. So why did he resort to a completely unnecessary measure on the very first day, one from which he must have known it would only create bad mood and rally almost everyone against him? That didn't make sense! Unless - there was more behind this. Much more than anyone else suspected.
