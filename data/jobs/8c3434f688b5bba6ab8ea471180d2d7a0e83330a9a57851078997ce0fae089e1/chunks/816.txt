Suddenly a strange noise was heard. It sounded like the howling of a wolf. Laura sighed softly and turned in her bed as if disturbed by this eerie howling that interrupted her sleep. At the same moment, the doorknob of her room's door was pushed down silently. With an almost imperceptible creaking sound, the door opened. A thin beam of light from the hallway fell into the room, moved across the wall where Laura’s bed stood, grew larger and larger until it finally illuminated Laura's face. Fabric rustled.
