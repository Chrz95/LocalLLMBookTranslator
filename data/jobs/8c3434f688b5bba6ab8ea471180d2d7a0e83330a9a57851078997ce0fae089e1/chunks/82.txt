"During the time when Sayelle Leander-Rüchlin was employed as the head of the economic section, it belonged to him," Laura remembered. Laura had first met Maximilian Longolius at a dinner in an exquisite restaurant where he had invited Sayelle and her and Lukas: a somewhat older, slightly worn-out type with receding black hair - Laura had noticed on the spot that they were dyed - dressed in elegant attire and wearing designer glasses. His handshake was as limp as a rabbit's paw, she shuddered at the memory. But what had been worst of all was how he had constantly cajoled Sayelle. His pig-like eyes had practically clung to her, and he had always smiled.
