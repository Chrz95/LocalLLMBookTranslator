Laura stared for a tiny moment, lost in thought. What does this mean? she wondered, but then to her own surprise, she noticed that she had already pulled the blanket aside and stood up from the bed. Without really wanting to, she got out of bed, slipped into her winter boots, put on the red parka over her pajamas, and stumbled drowsily towards the open door. A mysterious spell seemed to guide her steps without her being able to do anything about it. It was as if a strange force had taken hold of her.
