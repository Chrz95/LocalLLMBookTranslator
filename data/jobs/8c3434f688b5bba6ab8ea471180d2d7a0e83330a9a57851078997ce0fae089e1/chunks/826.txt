Something was off here, thought Laura. And suddenly it hit her: While her own footsteps were muted but still clearly audible, the teacher moved without making a single sound. Laura studied her more closely and had the impression that Miss Mary wasn't even using her legs! True, the teacher's cloak almost reached the ground, so neither legs nor feet could be seen, but even the heavy fabric of the cape showed no movement. No creasing, no folding, nothing. The slender teacher seemed to float and glided completely noiselessly up the stairs.
