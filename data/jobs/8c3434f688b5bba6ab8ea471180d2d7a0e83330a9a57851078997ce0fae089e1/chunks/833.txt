The moonlight flooded through the round ornamental window above the entrance portal and cast a pale light onto the painting on the opposite wall. Laura instinctively turned her head toward the picture. What she saw made her jump in fright. She stood rooted to the spot, holding her breath. For an instant, Laura did not know if she was truly awake or if everything was just a dream. On the canvas, only the young woman in white remained visible. With eyes that seemed infinitely sad, she stared unblinkingly at Laura. The large black wolf, which usually lay at her feet, had vanished without a trace!
