The narrow path ran alongside the main building of the castle. Laura's anorak glowed like a red signal light in the darkness of the night as she followed the teacher, who was twenty meters ahead and moving completely silently. The gravel crunched under Laura’s boots, while the cold, damp night air tickled her nose and smelled—no, unfortunately not after snow. It had been Kastor Dietrich who taught Laura that one could smell snow. In fact, the farmer had imparted so much to her. He had shown her how to pay attention to the signs of nature, from which the knowledgeable person can read a lot. But now all Laura smelled were moldy leaves and decaying wood.
