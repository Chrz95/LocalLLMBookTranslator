When the girl turned back to Miss Mary, she was just disappearing into the mist that drifted through the park. Laura quickened her pace. A few moments later, she too was enveloped by the gray haze. It obstructed her vision. She could no longer see Miss Mary and there were no other souls in sight. The fog grew denser. Ghostly shapes emerged from the bushes and shrubs that lined the path ahead. In the distance, a fox barked, and two winged shadows glided silently over Laura's head. The girl jumped but the shadows had already vanished again. Laura convinced herself it was only the pair of stone martens nesting in the old oak behind the gymnasium and hurried on.
