When she reached a fork in the road, she didn't know which direction to take. She had no idea where Miss Mary wanted her to go. But there was not a trace of the teacher anywhere, and so the girl stood uncertain for a moment before finally deciding to turn right. If Laura wasn't mistaken, this narrow path led to Professor Morgenstern's house, which lay far off from the main building in a quiet corner of the park. Perhaps that is where our excursion was heading?, Laura thought hesitantly and hurried on with a troubled heart.
