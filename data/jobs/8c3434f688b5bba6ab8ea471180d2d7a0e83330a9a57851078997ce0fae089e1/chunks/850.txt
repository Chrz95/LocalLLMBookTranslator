The fog was now so impenetrable that she could only see a few meters ahead. Only the gravel path before her was still visible; the bushes and shrubs of the park were hidden behind a gray veil. But the director's house did not appear, even though it should have been there by now. The path seemed to go on forever, leading deeper into the swirling mist. Laura realized then that she had lost her way. She no longer knew where she was. Fear rose in her, and although she knew this was completely nonsensical, she ran faster than ever before.
