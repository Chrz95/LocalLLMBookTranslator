The path through the marshlands seemed almost endless to Morwena. Although her two-horned steed had covered most of it at a gallop, it had felt much longer than usual because she had been thinking about Hellunyat all along with great longing. She could hardly wait to finally arrive there. When the roar of the Donnerfluss reached her ears, she hoped fervently that the river would appear before her any moment now. But to her disappointment, a long time passed until at last she reached the mighty stream.
