The sight of the new bridge compensated Morwena for her anxious anticipation of the past hours. Although it was in the middle of the night, the healer could already from a distance see that she had now been completed. Her hope for an shortcut had come true. Like a fulfilled promise, the wooden structure shimmered towards her under the bright light of both moons. Proud and bold, the bridge stretched itself across the gurgling waters. Thanks to its thick supports and sturdy planks, it appeared much more trustworthy than many hastily built bridges elsewhere that made travelers fear for their lives. It seemed destined to last forever.
