Morwena heard a shrill laughter above her, the laughter of a woman. Surprised, she raised her gaze and noticed a massive winged creature hovering over the Thunder River before it ascended into the night sky with powerful flaps of its mighty wings. Before Morwena could discern what was happening, she was swept against a rock that protruded from the water. As her head collided with the rock, she heard another loud crash. Then everything turned black around her, and she lost consciousness.
