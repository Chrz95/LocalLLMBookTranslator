At the stony fireplace at the front of the room, a large fire crackled. It illuminated part of the room and cast flickering shadows on the walls. Long logs burned in the embers, and the fragrant smell of dry resin filled Laura's nose. After only a short time her eyes had grown accustomed to the half-light. The room was sparsely and simply furnished. Simple wooden cupboards and some wooden chests and shelves stood against the walls, that was all. In the middle of the room stood a round table with just over two-and-a-half meters in diameter. At its center was an ornateless stone fire bowl, in which a strange flame burned. It was barely hand-sized but dazzlingly white and so bright that Laura had to squint when she looked into it.
