However - Laura Sayelle could sometimes understand: Her stepmother always took on so much for the weekends because that was the only time they could spend together. On other days, Laura and Lukas lived in the boarding school. Only during weekends and holidays did they live at their family's house in Hohenstadt. And somehow it seemed to Sayelle as if she felt obligated to spend this scarce time almost entirely with them, so that she always wanted to go on a monster program with them every time. But perhaps that was also just an annoying duty, because otherwise she would have long since understood that they had no interest in museums and concerts.
