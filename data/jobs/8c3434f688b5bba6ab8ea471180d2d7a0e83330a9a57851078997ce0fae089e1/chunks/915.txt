"Only the fewest people know," Aurelius Morgenstern whispered at the beginning, "that since the dawn of time a parallel world to our Earth has existed. It is much older than our planet and is called >Aventerra<. From this mysterious world of myths, which lies beyond human knowledge, good and evil once found their way to our Earth, because on Aventerra stand the warriors of light against the powers of darkness in an eternal struggle and fight fiercely for dominance."
