The forces of Good are led by Elysion, the Guardian of Light. At the head of the Dark Armies stands the Black Prince Borboron, who ceaselessly and with unceasing fury leads his countless followers into battle against the defenders of light, legions of dark warriors that never tire. For ages he has pursued but one goal: to kill the Guardian of Light and thereby bring about the final victory for the Dark Powers. Then will the rule of Eternity's Nothingness break upon us, and Aventerra will fall - and our Earth as well!
