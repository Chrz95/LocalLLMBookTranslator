"Yes, Laura. As I already said, Good and Evil once came from Aventerra into our world. At that time, both the forces of Light and the powers of Evil sent their representatives here so they could settle on Earth. Since then, there has also been an eternal struggle between Good and Evil in our world, though not in the pure and clearly recognizable form as on Aventerra. On our Earth, this confrontation usually takes place in secret, and people often cannot properly understand what it is all about. For that reason, it is important that some few are vigilant and keep their eyes open. This is the foremost task of us Watchers: to be alert and always on guard against Evil. Only when we can recognize our enemies' plans early enough, can we thwart them."
